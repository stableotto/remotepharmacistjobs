About Shields Health Solutions

Shields Health Solutions builds and runs specialty pharmacy programs inside health systems, rather than operating a retail chain of its own. It partners with health systems across the country and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Walgreens Boots Alliance. Remote roles here are weighted toward clinical specialty pharmacists and certified technicians doing refill coordination, adherence outreach and prior-authorization support for health-system patients.

Licensure expectation

Supports health system partners across many states. If you are weighing which licences to add, our state licensure guide covers reciprocity and what each board asks for.
