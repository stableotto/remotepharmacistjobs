About Precision Medicine Group

Precision Medicine Group is a life-sciences services organization operating through two arms: Precision for Medicine, which does biomarker-driven R&D, and Precision AQ, its market-access and commercialization group formerly known as Precision Value & Health. Pharmacy-adjacent openings here are market-access and healthcare advertising project roles rather than clinical practice. Both of its job boards post under this one organization.

Also known as

Precision AQ, Precision Value & Health, Precision for Medicine. Job listings may appear under any of these names.
