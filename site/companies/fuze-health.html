About Fuze Health

Fuze Health is a digital pharmacy and at-home care company formed by combining Truepill, LetsGetChecked and Alto Pharmacy. Its FuzeRx arm provides white-labeled digital pharmacy services for healthcare partners. Remote consulting pharmacist roles support that pharmacy operation and are usually tied to a specific state licence.
