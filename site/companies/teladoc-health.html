About Teladoc Health

Teladoc Health is one of the largest virtual-care providers in the United States. Its clinical pharmacist roles are fully remote by the nature of the business, supporting virtual care delivery across a national member base — which usually means multi-state licensure.

Licensure expectation

National virtual care delivery typically requires licensure in multiple states. If you are weighing which licences to add, our state licensure guide covers reciprocity and what each board asks for.
