Viatris is a global healthcare company uniquely positioned to bridge the traditional divide between generics and brands, combining the best of both to more holistically address healthcare needs globally. We have been included on number of award lists that demonstrate the impact we are making.
 

Position Title: Pharmacy Sales Representative

Location: Melbourne/Vic North & Tas North
Employment: 12 Month Maternity Leave contract


Every day, we rise to the challenge to make a difference and here’s how the Business Advisor role will make an impact, the key responsibilities for this role include:

    • Maintain the designated territory ensuring continuation of optimum service to 1st line accounts;
    • Exceeding net sales target of dispensary and OTC pharmacy products within a defined territory of supporting customers and acquisition targets;
    • Maintain loyalty of 1st line accounts through building close relationships to ensure their requirements are being met by the company’s products where possible;
    • Execute agreed business tactics to optimize substitution opportunities;
    • Design & execute quarterly business plans which include targets, action plan & outcomes for each account;
    • Facilitate a 1st class sales experience using business insights, analytics & innovative deal-level strategies;
    • Acknowledge & overcome customer concerns. Take ownership of territory issues & provide appropriate solutions. Escalate to Regional Business Manager if required;
    • Consistently record & assess call outcomes in call notes within the required system.

The minimum qualifications and skillset required for this role is:

    • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Pharmacy, Science or Sales;
    • At least 1 years experience working within Australia’s generic market;
    • 1-2 years’ experience in B2B complex sales environments;
    • Proven track record of success in exceeding sales targets;
    • Excellent communication skills (written and verbal);
    • Must be eligible to work in Australia (Australian Citizen or appropriate Visa holder).
