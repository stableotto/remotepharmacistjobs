Job Summary:

SmithRx is looking for a motivated Senior Clinical Review Operations Pharmacist to serve as a clinical subject matter expert and key operational driver within our Prior Authorization team. This individual contributor role is pivotal in maintaining high-quality clinical reviews, ensuring adherence to turnaround times, and delivering exceptional member experiences. Your primary focus will be building tools to support prior authorization evaluations, driving process improvements, mentoring peer pharmacists on clinical protocols, and serving as a key cross-functional innovator  to scale our clinical operations.

What you will do:

  • Process & Technology Optimization: Identify operational process gaps and champion technology-driven solutions to improve throughput, scalability, and workflow efficiency.
  • Clinical Mentorship & Quality Assurance: Serve as a clinical peer lead, providing guidance, coaching, and onboarding support to fellow pharmacists to foster clinical consistency and quality.
  • Complex Escalations: Act as a senior operational contact for complex clinical cases, escalated coverage determinations, and appeals.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with internal teams to troubleshoot eligibility questions, claims adjudication, and benefit design nuances.
  • Clinical Policy & P&T Committee Support: Collaborate with clinical leadership and the Pharmacy and Therapeutics (P&T) Committee to help develop, refine, and review clinical criteria for medications requiring prior authorization.
  • Performance & SLAs: Consistently meet or exceed individual target KPIs and turn-around-time (TAT) SLAs to maintain seamless operating standards.
  • Change Champion: Drive adoption of new technology, workflow tools, and updated clinical protocols across the Clinical Review team.

What you will bring to SmithRx:

  • 5+ years of health plan or Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) experience, specifically involved in prior authorization review and clinical operations.
  • Demonstrated expertise as a clinical subject matter expert with a track record of driving process improvements or technology-driven efficiencies.
  • Active Pharmacist License (unrestricted) required.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) required.
  • Strong proficiency with Mac, MS Office, and G-Suite platforms.
  • High degree of empathy with excellent active listening and communication skills to clearly convey complex clinical information to members, providers, and internal stakeholders.
  • Proven self-starter with superior multitasking, time management, and problem-solving abilities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Deep understanding of drug product/dose calculations, claim adjudication rules, and general pharmacy dispensing processes.
  • Adaptability to evolving clinical guidelines, changing protocols, and new operational technology.
