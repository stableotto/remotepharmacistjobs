🚀 Your Impact: Patient Engagement Specialist

As a Patient Engagement Specialist, you will serve as a vital connection between patients, pharmacies, providers, and internal teams. Through inbound and outbound patient interactions, you'll help ensure patients receive medications on time, navigate pharmacy processes, and maintain continuity of care.

Key Responsibilities

📞 Proactively coordinate medication refills, including data entry and pharmacy claim adjudication

📞 Make outbound patient refill calls and assist patients through the complete refill process

📞 Answer, resolve, and appropriately triage inbound patient and provider inquiries

📞 Clarify prescription orders with pharmacists and/or clinicians when appropriate

📞 Identify medications requiring special handling, including refrigerated or hazardous medications

📞 Manage, organize, and update patient and pharmacy information within database applications

📞 Assist pharmacies with prescription claim adjudication and resolution of claim processing errors

📞 Respond to questions regarding prescription coverage, fulfillment status, and pharmacy services

📞 Serve as a key point of contact between patients, pharmacies, pharmacy liaisons, clinical providers, and internal operational teams

📞 Identify trends, resolve challenges, recommend improvements, and support operational excellence initiatives

📞 Participate in process improvement efforts that enhance patient experience, productivity, quality, and service standards

📞 Maintain strict confidentiality and compliance with all applicable policies and regulations

📞 Stay current on industry and organizational knowledge through ongoing education and professional development

📞 Support business and health system partner needs with flexible scheduling, including start times as early as 7:45 AM EST / 8:00 AM EST

📞 Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned
