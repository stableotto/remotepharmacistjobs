Job Summary:

Joining SmithRx’s clinical team, the Clinical Review Pharmacist plays a key role in enhancing clinical offerings and improving outcomes for our members and clients. Operating in a collaborative and fast-paced environment, this position pairs clinical expertise with service excellence to review prior authorization requests, support clinical programs, and offer guidance to providers, pharmacies, and members.

What you will do:

  • Review and make timely, evidence-based decisions on prior authorizations and appeals using clinical guidelines and benefit criteria.
  • Ensure compliance with federal, state, and internal regulations across applicable lines of businesses .
  • Accurately document clinical decisions and maintain thorough records in accordance with regulatory and accreditation (URAC/NCQA) utilization review standards.
  • Provide clear, empathetic support to members, prescribers, and pharmacies by responding to clinical inquiries, demonstrating professionalism, active listening, and a patient-centered approach.
  • Stay current with clinical prescribing guidelines, internal policies, and regulatory changes, and apply them to daily responsibilities.
  • Follow all internal procedures, job aids, and HIPAA guidelines to protect patient privacy and data security.
  • Identify and report potential fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA).
  • Support training and development of new and existing team members as needed.
  • Assist leadership with special projects, process improvements, and operational initiatives

What you will bring to SmithRx:

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD.) or Bachelor of Science in Pharmacy (B.S. Pharm) from an ACPE-accredited school of pharmacy
  • Active, unrestricted, pharmacist license in state of residence
  • Minimum of 2 years of pharmacy practice experience
  • Strong communication, writing, and organizational skills
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a high-volume, metric-driven environment
  • General understanding of drug information, clinical guidelines, formulary design, and utilization management strategies
  • Flexible and adaptable to changes
  • Availability to work after hours, weekends, and holidays on a rotating or fixed schedule

Preferred Qualifications:

  • One year of prior authorization or utilization management experience
  • One year experience in managed care or pharmacy benefit management (PBM)
  • Direct experience conducting prior authorization reviews, clinical appeals, formulary exceptions, step therapy evaluations, and quantity limit reviews
  • Experience in Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Drive)
