Senior Art Director (Pharmaceutical)

Senior · $68,000 - $91,000/yr · Remote, EST United States
RemoteSeniorPosted Nov 19, 2026Lever

We’re looking for a Senior Art Director to bring big ideas to life for our pharmaceutical clients. You’ll concept and design integrated work across digital, print, social and video, partnering closely with copywriters to turn strategy and clinical insight into creative that’s smart, beautiful and compliant. You’re hands-on, confident presenting your thinking, and ready to own projects from first sketch to final file. You will report to an ACD, Art.

This role is open to fully remote candidates in the United States with a preference for the Eastern time zone.

What you'll do

  • Concept and art direct integrated campaigns and tactics across digital, print, social, video and omnichannel
  • Partner with copywriters to develop ideas that balance storytelling with scientific accuracy and regulatory compliance
  • Translate clinical data, customer insights and brand strategy into clear, compelling visual work
  • Own your projects from concept through final production, keeping craft and consistency high
  • Present your work and rationale to internal teams, and to clients when needed
  • Interpret feedback from clients, MLR and creative leadership, and solve design challenges thoughtfully
  • Manage multiple projects at once and hit tight regulatory deadlines
  • Bring fresh ideas, techniques and tools to the team, and push the work beyond what’s safe

What you bring

  • 3–5 years of art direction or design experience at an advertising agency
  • Experience with pharmaceutical or healthcare clients across HCP and/or DTC
  • A strong portfolio showing campaign thinking and concepts as well as polished execution across mediums
  • Working knowledge of pharmaceutical regulations and how MLR review shapes creative
  • Fluency in Adobe Creative Suite. Motion, illustration or Figma skills are a plus
  • Confident communication and presentation skills
  • A collaborative, low-ego approach and a willingness to take feedback and run with it
  • Bachelor’s degree in graphic design, visual communications or a related field

What this role is not
This isn’t a production-only role. You’ll be expected to concept and bring a point of view, not just execute someone else’s layouts. It’s also not a people-management role. You’ll be a hands-on creative who partners with copy and grows under the guidance of your ACD and Art Supervisors.

 

What you’ll get:

  • Remote, for real: P10 has been fully remote for 15 years, by design. No office mandates, no return-to-office surprises
  • Time to recharge: 15 days of paid vacation (plus one more day for every year with us), unlimited flex and sick time, and a company holiday calendar
  • Health coverage: medical, dental and vision through Cigna, with P10 covering 80% of employee medical premiums and 100% of employee dental and vision
  • Peace of mind: life insurance, plus short- and long-term disability coverage for everyone
  • Planning ahead: a retirement savings plan with a company match of up to 2% (Betterment in the US, Wealthsimple in Canada)
  • Set up for success: a laptop shipped to you before day one, plus a home office stipend to make your space your own
  • Real connection: an annual all-company offsite, plus a Slack culture full of shout-outs, side channels and people who actually know each other
  • A flat, hands-on team: small teams, fewer approval layers and leaders who are in the work with you

 

$68,000 - $91,000 a year

The environment

P10 is remote-first, always has been. This role is open to candidates in the United States, with a preference for Eastern time zone. You'll be working inside a structure flat enough that your creative vision actually shapes client work.
