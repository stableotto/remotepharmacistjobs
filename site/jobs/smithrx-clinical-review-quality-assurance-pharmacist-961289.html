Job Summary:

SmithRx is looking for a motivated Clinical Review Quality Assurance Pharmacist to develop and maintain a quality audit program for the Clinical Review Team. This role is pivotal to the prior authorization team for ensuring high quality reviews, adherence to regulatory requirements, and delivery of exceptional member, provider and employee experiences.

What you will do:

  • Responsible for developing, defining, maintaining and instituting a prior authorization quality assurance program for the Pharmacy Technician and Pharmacist clinical review function
  • Responsible for summarizing quality assurance activities and presenting to leadership and the Clinical Review Team
  • Responsible for developing, maintaining and conducting an interrater reliability program
  • Monitors clinical appeals to further enhance the prior authorization program
  • Collaborate with Pharmacists within the Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee and Criteria Review Committee process and participate in development of clinical criteria for medications requiring prior authorization
  • Identifies opportunities for technology enhancements, process improvement or coaching for team members
  • Collaborate with Clinical Review Business Analyst to support system enhancements
  • Support Policy and Procedure development and maintenance
  • Become a subject matter expert on department standards and processes

What you will bring to SmithRx:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) required
  • Active Pharmacist License required
  • 5+ years of health plan or pharmacy benefit manager experience, specifically involved in prior authorization review required
  • 2+ years of experience conducting prior authorization quality audits required
  • 2+ years of experience designing and developing quality assurance best practices in a startup environment highly desired. 
  • Proficiency in Mac, MS Office, G-Suite required
  • Active listening, conversational speaking skills, with a high degree of empathy
  • Self-starter with ability to multitask, prioritize and manage time effectively
  • Ability to communicate clearly, present complex information to members, clients, pharmacies, providers, and other teams internally
  • Flexibility and ability to adapt to changes and updates to processes and protocols
  • Demonstrate accurate drug product/dose calculations for appropriate claim adjudication and knowledge of general pharmacy and drug product information for the prescription filling process
