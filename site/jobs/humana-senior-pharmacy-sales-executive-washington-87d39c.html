Senior · $84,700 - $115,500/yr · Remote Washington

Become a part of our caring community
 

The Senior Pharmacy Sales Executive leverages targeted geographic analysis, engages with Physician offices to influence physicians to route patients' specialty drug prescriptions to Humana Specialty Pharmacy for fulfillment. The Senior Pharmacy Sales Executive focuses on increasing orders to Humana Pharmacy, resulting in increased prescriptions and revenues.

As a Senior Pharmacy Sales Executive you will spend time building relationships on front office to back office medical clinic sales calls within the territory. You will provide staff education and information on options for patient use of Specialty Pharmacy medications.

Main Responsibilities:

  • Manage a sales territory that includes CenterWell, a Humana-owned brand offering Primary Care, Home Health, and Pharmacy services.  

  • The sales territory includes Seattle, Spokane, Takoma, Bellingham, Lakewood Washington areas.

  • Build relationships with front and back-office staff by conducting sales calls within the territory. 

  • Provide staff education and information on options for patient use of Specialty Pharmacy medications. 

  • Become a knowledgeable resource regarding specialty pharmacy medications and Humana pharmacy benefits. 

  • Educate clinics on the several patient benefits for using Humana Mail Order pharmacy - lower cost, easier approvals, resource assistance and a more comprehensive cycle of care.

  • You will become a knowledgeable resource regarding specialty pharmacy medications and Humana pharmacy benefits.

You will receive a competitive base salary and bonus, full benefits, an amazing work culture and an opportunity to have a positive impact on the health of Humana members. You will also receive a company vehicle will be provided for travel.
