Clinical Pharmacist Consultant

Micromedex

Micromedex by Merative is a trusted clinical decision support solution used by clinicians in thousands of hospitals, health systems, payers, and government agencies worldwide. For over 50 years, we’ve delivered evidence-based drug, toxicology, and condition information to help clinicians make confident, timely decisions and educate patients at the point of care.

Today, Micromedex is evolving. With a modernized homepage and AI-powered search, clinicians can now find precise answers faster—supported by rigorously validated, evidence-based content. Our portfolio includes drug reference, IV compatibility, pediatric dosing, toxicology databases, and integrated calculators, all accessible via web and mobile. By combining authoritative content with intuitive, AI-enhanced tools, Micromedex empowers healthcare organizations to improve medication safety, reduce adverse events, and deliver better patient outcomes.

We are seeking a dedicated and knowledgeable Pharmacist Clinical Consultant to join our team. This role involves providing expert clinical support and guidance to healthcare professionals using Micromedex solutions. The Pharmacist Clinical Consultant will have a role in both pre-sale and post-sale customer engagements. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in clinical pharmacy and a passion for improving patient care through evidence-based practices. 

The Pharmacist Clinical Consultant serves as a strategic clinical advisor across pre-sale and post-sale engagements, partnering with Sales, Account Management, Product, and Editorial teams to influence buying decisions, drive customer adoption, and inform product direction. This role blends deep clinical pharmacy expertise with consultative selling, market insight, and thought leadership to advance medication safety and clinical decision support outcomes.

Preferred Requirements

  • Board certification in a pharmacy specialty (e.g., BCPS, BCACP) or PGY2 in Informatics or Management / Administration.
  • Experience in health IT, clinical decision support, informatics, or industry roles
  • Experience supporting enterprise healthcare organizations or complex sales cycles.

Education & Experience Requirements

  • Professional doctorate degree (PharmD)  
  • Post-graduate residency training (PGY1)
  • Minimum of 5 years of clinical pharmacy practice experience, preferably in a hospital or healthcare setting.
  • Strong knowledge of pharmacotherapy.  

Pre-Sale Essential Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provides expert clinical guidance on the positioning of Micromedex tools and resources to sales and account executives to progress new logo or expansion opportunities to closure. 
  • Prepares and delivers clinical demonstrations for individual customer meetings, during conferences or tradeshow events that convey the overall utility and value of Micromedex.  
  • Answers clinical questions about solution applications and new features to prospects and to various groups within new and existing customer accounts. 
  • Builds relationships with clinical stakeholders during evaluations to gain support for Micromedex selection.    
  • Maintains deep competitive insights that support sales strategies in differentiating Micromedex. 
  • Acts as a subject matter expert for RFI/RFP responses, clinical questionnaires, and proposal development 
  • Partners with Sales to shape opportunity strategy, including clinical qualification, objection handling, and competitive displacement. 
  • Engages directly with pharmacy, medication safety, informatics, and executive stakeholders to align Micromedex solutions with organizational priorities and regulatory requirements. 

Post Sale Essential Duties & Responsibilities

  • Collaborates with Account Managers and Account Executives to support education and training for customers that maximize the use of Micromedex solutions.  
  • Supports user groups or other types of showcase events that drive preference for Micromedex solutions.  
  • Addresses clinical questions for customers leveraging deep product, content and editorial policy knowledge in collaboration with other SMEs as required. 
  • Guides customers in optimizing Micromedex use to support medication safety initiatives, accreditation readiness, and clinical quality goals. 
  • Partners with Account Managers and Account Executives to identify expansion opportunities by aligning additional Micromedex capabilities to evolving clinical and operational needs. 
  • Serves as a trusted clinical advisor for complex customer questions related to content interpretation, evidence standards, and editorial policy. 

Basic Qualifications   

  • Strong, influential, written and verbal communication and presentation skills.  
  • Methodical and detail-oriented planner and problem-solver; ability to identify themes, risks, and recommend solutions.  
  • Personal interest in health information technology and clinical decision support. 
  • Ability to translate AI-driven features into clinically meaningful and responsible use cases for customers. 
  • Ability to travel up to 50%.
