Based in San Francisco, Arine is a rapidly growing healthcare technology and clinical services company with a mission to ensure individuals receive the safest and most effective treatments for their unique and evolving healthcare needs. 

Frequently, medications cause more harm than good. Incorrect drugs and doses costs the US healthcare system over $528 billion in waste, avoidable harm, and hospitalizations each year. Arine is redefining what excellent healthcare looks like by solving these issues through our software platform (SaaS). We combine cutting edge data science, machine learning, AI, and deep clinical expertise to introduce a patient-centric view to medication management, and develop and deliver personalized care plans on a massive scale for patients and their care teams.

Arine is committed to improving the lives and health of complex patients that have an outsized impact on healthcare costs and have traditionally been difficult to identify and address. These patients face numerous challenges including complicated prescribing issues across multiple medications and providers, medication challenges with many chronic diseases, and patient issues with access to care. Backed by leading healthcare investors and collaborating with top healthcare organizations and providers, we deliver recommendations and facilitate clinical interventions that lead to significant, measurable health improvements for patients and cost savings for customers.

The Role:

We are looking for a Lead Clinical Pharmacist to assist in leading one of the clinical teams that uses the Arine platform to deliver excellent outcomes for our customers. 

The Lead Clinical Pharmacist is part of a team of clinicians, analysts, clinical support staff, and trainees utilizing Arine’s software platform to help optimize patient outcomes for our clients. The purpose of the Lead Clinical Pharmacist position is to assist in managing a patient engagement program that achieves four and five-star results across a variety of Medicare Part C and Part D measures. The position leads and oversees expert level medication therapy consultation to patients and providers, collaborates with the Engineering team and leadership to improve Arine’s software platform, and supervises a team of Pharmacists who are directly reviewing medication profiles and counseling patients, interacting with other health professionals to help improve patient outcomes. The Lead Clinical Pharmacist partners with the Clinical Support Lead to achieve results by setting a strategic approach with Arine leadership and with other Clinical Care Leads to drive excellent medication outcomes and by engaging client teams and Arine Customer Success to align expectations and communicate roadblocks and progress. 

This role encompasses all of Arine's Clinical Care team activities. Lead Clinical Pharmacists will be asked to:

  • Support oversight of goals, progress, and resource allocation of an Arine full-service clinical project
  • Develop intervention strategies to optimize medication regimens to improve health outcomes (example, decrease hospitalizations, cost, and morbidity),  support CMS quality measures (example adherence, SUPD and SPC), and other established goals
  • Manage team productivity and timelines to ensure goals are met 
  • Ensure quality assurance standards are met
  • Develop new workflows, best practices, and standards to drive continuous improvement in health outcomes, efficiencies, and insights to improve our product 
  • Prioritize project needs and be able to communicate the magnitude of potential impacts when escalating product asks, staffing requests, etc
  • Represent Arine’s Clinical Care team at customer meetings, as well as lead internal staff meetings
  • Hire, train, and schedule team members and help lead in the growth and development of the team
  • Manage and conduct performance reviews for assigned team members
  • Respond to questions by patients, providers, and other professional personnel and team members
  • Comply with all company policies and state and federal rules and regulations
