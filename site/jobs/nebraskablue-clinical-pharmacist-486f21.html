Clinical Pharmacist

Mid-level · Remote flexibility for candidates in FL, IA, KS, M
RemoteMid-levelPosted Jun 01, 2026Workday

At Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska, we are a mission-driven organization dedicated to championing the health and well-being of our members and the communities we serve.

Our team is the power behind that promise. And, as the industry rapidly evolves and we seek ways to optimize business processes and customer experiences, there’s no greater time for forward-thinking professionals like you to join us in delivering on it! As a member of Team Blue, you’ll find purpose, opportunities and the support you need to build a meaningful career and make a powerful impact in our community.

This position will be the expert of the team on clinical issues in conjunction with the Director of Pharmacy and other staff pharmacists. This position will be responsible for working in a collaborative culture focused on designing, implementing, maintaining and measuring pharmacy-related utilization management programs. This includes but is not limited to development of work flows, criteria development and ensuring that URAC accreditation is maintained. Additionally, supporting Medicare Advantage processes, outreach, and tasks that require clinical discussion and presentation.

Candidates applying to this position may be hybrid or remote and can live in one of the following states: Florida, Iowa, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, and Texas. 

What you'll do:

  • Create and Maintain Clinical Criteria for applicable Utilization Management (UM) programs: Develop and maintain clinical criteria (access to Prime standard and BCBSNE specific versions); Develop and maintain criteria process flows; Develop and maintain physician fax forms and other modes of communication; Utilize predictive modeling tool to intervene with high-risk members for identified disease states; Provide clinical updates as necessary to internal staff and physician reviewers; Performs highly complex research, analysis and develops recommendations for UM programs & plan design changes.
  • Responsible for Operational workflows and Completion of UM reviews: Clinical oversight of first level review process; Clinical review of utilization management reviews; Perform clinical support for Pharmacy Technician and other staff; Utilize reporting tools to create efficiencies by reviewing medical and pharmacy claims data; Support physician reviewers with information required to efficiently review non-approvable claims; Develop Decision Tree review process when applicable.
  • Maintain URAC accreditation: Work with internal staff to ensure that URAC accreditation is maintained; The pharmacist will work within their scope of licensure and unless expressly allowed by state or federal law or regulation, are located in a state or territory of the United States when conducting drug utilization review. The clinical pharmacists may not conduct peer clinical review non-certifications for drug utilization management, if prohibited by state utilization management laws. The clinical pharmacists may not conduct peer clinical review non-certifications for drug utilization management if the requesting party specifically requests a clinical peer. Measure quality assurance reporting, turnaround time for urgent and non-urgent reviews and quantitative assessment of UM reviews by program.
  • Support Medicare Advantage processes, outreach, presentations, and tasks that require clinical direction and support. This includes, but not limited to, clinical presentations to internal and external stakeholders, providers, provider offices, and other customers.

To be considered for this position, you must have:

  • Doctor of Pharmacy required from Accredited US College of Pharmacy.
  • An active, current, and unrestricted license or certification to practice pharmacy in Nebraska.

The strongest candidates for this position will also possess:

  • Experience with PBM operations, Medicare Advantage, and/or clinical program development and maintenance.
  • Managed Care Pharmacy Residency or 3-4 years of experience in Managed Care Pharmacy industry.

We strongly believe that diversity of experience, perspective and background will lead to a better workplace for our employees and a better product for our customers and members.
