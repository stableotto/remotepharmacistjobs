Pharmacist I

Entry-level · $94,100 - $164,800/yr · Dayton WFH
RemoteEntry-levelPosted Jul 30, 2026Workday

Job Summary:

Pharmacist I is responsible for supporting the department, operational strategies, goals and vision. Pharmacists shall utilize pharmaceutical and clinical expertise to review Prior Authorizations requests within a timely manner, to promptly serve our CareSource members.

Essential Functions:

  • Supports and participates in Prior Authorization (PA) Reviews by documenting, implementing and utilizing the following: Formulary Management guidelines, Utilization Management programs, CareSource Administrative and Operational policies and procedures, and/or other clinical programs
  • Execute critical thinking and decision processes when evaluating PA Reviews for Pharmacy and Medical Benefit medications
  • Conduct PA Reviews for Multi-State Medicaid and Marketplace lines of business
  • Provide written communications to members and providers upon decisioning of PA requests
  • Engage Providers in “Peer to Peer” discussions, and Clinical Consults, as requested
  • Assist Pharmacy PA call center staff
  • Provide support and collaborate with pharmacy technicians to ensure operational efficiencies
  • Adhere to Turn Around Time (TAT) requirements, per Line of Business
  • Observe Operational Productivity Goals and Metrics
  • Maintain exceptional quality
  • Cross-collaborate with Departmental and Organizational Teams
  • Perform any other job duties as requested

Education and Experience:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Pharmacy is required; Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) is preferred
  • Minimum of three (3) years of retail pharmacy experience is preferred
  • Minimum of one (1) year managed care or Medicare experience is preferred

Competencies, Knowledge and Skills:

  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint 
  • Familiarity with the healthcare field, including Insurer, PBM, Specialty or Retail Pharmacy and Provider
  • Understanding Managed Care and/or Retail Pharmacy operations with respect to Medicaid, Marketplace and /or Medicare
  • Proficient in medical terminology, medication names, generics and biosimilars
  • Application of Clinical guidelines, policies, and decision trees/tools
  • Understanding the pharmacist’s clinical role in the care of members; utilization of Clinical expertise
  • Strong written and verbal Communication skills, including use of proper grammar in print and e-mail communications
  • Ability to simplify clinical language for lay persons
  • Critical listening, thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Organized and learns quickly
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment
  • Time management skills
  • Use of proper phone etiquette
  • Ability to work independently and within a team environment

Licensure and Certification:

  • Current, unrestricted Registered Pharmacist (RPh) licensure is required
  • Ability to acquire additional State License(s) in multiple states, as required

Working Conditions:

  • General office or Remote work environment; may be required to sit or stand for extended periods of time
