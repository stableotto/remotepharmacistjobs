Senior Clinical Pharmacist

Senior · $107,700 - $199,300/yr · Remote-FL
RemoteSeniorPosted Nov 06, 2026Workday

Position Purpose: Define and develop standard and custom formularies for complex or specialized medications for assigned plan


  • Develop and implement medication therapy management programs.

  • Evaluate program performance, recommend changes and ensure successful implementation of changes

  • Ensure and evaluate quality controls and implement opportunities for performance improvement

  • Provide clinical guidance and support to various departments and address clinical related questions

  • For Clinical Programs (Operational Excellence):

  • Support ongoing evaluation of clinical program initiatives promoting quality, safety and cost of care opportunity

  • Support oversight, development and expansion of clinical pharmacy programs and initiatives by assessing utilization trends and relevant data and literature

  • Analyze pharmacy data and trends needed to drive positive member outcomes

  • Ensure and develop strategic relationships supporting organizational strategic initiatives

  • Performs other duties as assigned

  • Complies with all policies and standards

Education/Experience: Bachelor’s degree or advanced degree (Pharm.D., M.S.) in pharmacy. 4+ years of mail order, retail, hospital, managed care pharmacy experience or pharmacy residency program experience.

Licenses/Certifications: Current state’s Pharmacist license with no restrictions
May be required to obtain additional state licensure based on business needs

For Clinical Programs (Operational Excellence): Support clinical program outcome analysis and evaluation across populations of patients in order to improve disease state outcomes and improve medication related safety. Support and assist in the development and analysis of various clinical programs to drive strategic initiatives across lines of business including Commercial, Medicaid, Medicare Part D and the Health Insurance Marketplace.

Pay Range: $107,700.00 - $199,300.00 per year
