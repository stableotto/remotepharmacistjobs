Pharmacy Service Excellence Issue Management Specialist

Mid-level · $54,500 - $87,300/yr · Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Jun 15, 2026Workday

Job Summary:

Pharmacy Service Excellence Issue Management Specialist is responsible to oversee the daily flow of

Pharmacy related issues and/or intakes.

Essential Functions:

  • Works closely with the Pharmacy Business Liaison and Quality Specialist to maintain status updates of the weekly Operations and Prioritization logs
  • Works with the Issues Management tracking system
  • Informs Pharmacy Leadership of pressing issues and escalates accordingly
  • Ability to train staff on the use of issues tracking software and supply updates to training documentation as necessary.
  • Coordinate departmental deliverable calendars to help ensure that the team is aware of impeding Regulatory, Accreditation, and/or Project deadlines
  • Helps with facilitating of data and requirements gathering for projects, Accreditation and Regulatory Audits as needed
  • Create, track, and facilitate the submission of Marketing Communication Request Forms (CRFs) and other pharmacy-related communications
  • Perform any other job duties as requested

Education and Experience:

  • High School Diploma or equivalent years relevant work experience is required
  • Three (3) years retail, hospital or other pharmacy experience is required
  • One (1) year managed care, PBM or other relevant experience is required

Competencies, Knowledge and Skills:

  • Computer proficient with knowledge in a “Windows” environment
  • Beginners level Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Excel
  • Professional level e-mail and communication skills
  • Familiarity with medical terminology and medication names
  • Excellent listening and thinking skills
  • Good decision making, problem solving and trouble shooting skills with strong attention to detail
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Ability to work independently and within a team environment
  • Familiarity of the healthcare field
  • Good time management skills
  • Customer service orientated

Licensure and Certification:

  • Pharmacy Technician Certification (CPhT) is preferred

Working Conditions:

  • General office environment; may be required to sit or stand for extended periods of time
  • Position requires the ability to work a standard business day shift, with some flexibility as needed

Compensation Range:

$54,500.00 - $87,300.00
