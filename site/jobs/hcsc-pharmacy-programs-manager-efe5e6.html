Job Summary

Provides comprehensive pharmacy network oversight and manages day-to-day operations with external pharmacy benefit managers across all Medicare plans. Continuously monitors network pharmacies to ensure pricing setup accuracy and maintain strict operational quality standards. Drives PBM vendor performance and operational integrity through claims research, rigorous testing of benefit design implementations, and direct oversight of PBM mail order and customer service functions. Ensures strict compliance with all Medicare, Medicaid, and enterprise regulatory standards.

Job Requirements:

  • Previous experience with pharmacy networks and/or health plans
  • Project management experience.
  • Verbal and written communication skills.
  • Flexible and ability to perform under pressure.
  • PC proficiency including Word and Excel and database experience.

This role is Remote in Nashville and requires working in East Coast Time Zone
