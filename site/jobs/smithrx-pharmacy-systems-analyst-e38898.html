Job Summary:

The Pricing Systems Analyst will be the architect and the builder behind SmithRx’s pricing models and bid data infrastructure. This role focuses on the back-end development, refinement, and enhancement of our proprietary pricing tools using SQL, Tableau, and Looker. You will act as the bridge between the Proposals teams and our Data, Engineering, and Operational departments, ensuring our data pipelines are robust, our models are accurate, and our pricing pathways are cutting-edge.

While focused on development, this role remains a critical subject matter expert, providing ad-hoc support and direct contribution on high-complexity submissions when the pipeline requires support or deep technical intervention.

What you will do:

  • Design, build, and refine complex pricing models in SQL and Looker to automate and enhance the accuracy of client savings projections.
  • Translate business requirements from stakeholders into technical specifications for the development and validation of our Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) in collaboration with the Data Analytics and Engineering groups.
  • Coordinate directly with Data and Engineering teams to ensure pricing logic is aligned with broader company data architecture and product roadmaps.
  • Contribute to the evolution of the Pricing Analytics codebase, adhering to best practices for development, rigorous audit protocols, and code reviews to ensure "client-ready" accuracy across all automated tools.
  • Maintain a transparent project roadmap for pricing tool enhancements and create detailed technical documentation for all pipelines and internal logic.
  • Provide "Tier 3" technical support to the Submission Pod, stepping in for highly complex ad-hoc analyses or during peak volume periods to ensure queue stability.
  • Execute deep-dive analyses and investigations into large datasets and provide clear insights on findings.
  • Communicate clearly with internal stakeholders and external partners through email, Slack, phone, video call, and Jira.
  • Develop a complete understanding of reporting needs and work cross-functionally to scope projects and develop self-service BI reporting and automation whenever relevant.
  • Continuously analyze and evaluate existing reporting processes, identifying inefficiencies or gaps, and proactively deploying creative solutions to enhance operational effectiveness.

What you will bring to SmithRx:

  • Experience: 3-5 years in a Data Analytics or Pricing focused role
  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a quantitative field (Engineering, Data Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, or equivalent).
  • Advanced proficiency in SQL (complex joins, CTEs, optimization) and experience with Looker (LookML), Tableau, or similar BI tools.
  • Familiarity with GitHub/Version Control and project management tools (e.g., Jira) is highly preferred.
  • Industry Knowledge: PBM or Healthcare experience is a significant plus, specifically regarding pharmacy claims data.
  • Communication: The ability to take a "messy" business requirement from a Sales Lead and translate it into a clean, repeatable technical specification.
  • Soft Skills: Highly self-motivated, adaptable, and willing to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Attention to Detail: You take pride in the accuracy of your formulas and the cleanliness of your code.
  • Leadership: Capable of facilitating deep technical discussions with Engineers while being able to translate the "why" to non-technical stakeholders.
