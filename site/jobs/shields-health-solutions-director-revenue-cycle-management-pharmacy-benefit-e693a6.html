Director, Revenue Cycle Management - Pharmacy Benefit

Position Summary

Shields Health Solutions is seeking an experienced Director, Specialty Pharmacy Revenue Cycle Management to lead and optimize revenue cycle operations supporting our growing network of health-system specialty pharmacies.

This leader will be responsible for the performance, scalability, and continuous improvement of specialty pharmacy revenue cycle functions, including pharmacy claims adjudication, PBM reimbursement, accounts receivable management, collections, cash application, payment reconciliation, claim resolution, and audit readiness.

The ideal candidate brings deep expertise within specialty pharmacy reimbursement and pharmacy benefit revenue cycle operations, including extensive experience working with pharmacy claims, PBMs, payer contracting structures, reimbursement methodologies, claim rejections, reversals and rebills, and complex accounts receivable management.

Partnering closely with Finance, Accounting, Pharmacy Operations, IT, Compliance, and health-system stakeholders, this leader will drive operational excellence, improve cash realization, strengthen financial controls, and develop scalable processes that support Shields' continued growth.

What You'll Do

Lead Specialty Pharmacy Revenue Cycle Operations

  • Direct specialty pharmacy revenue cycle operations including pharmacy claims billing, adjudication follow-up, collections, cash application, accounts receivable management, claim rejection resolution, and payment reconciliation.
  • Ensure timely and accurate reimbursement from commercial payers, government payers, and pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs).
  • Monitor accounts receivable performance, aging trends, unresolved balances, and reimbursement exceptions while leading corrective action efforts.
  • Oversee the identification and resolution of payer and PBM underpayments, overpayments, recoupments, and reimbursement discrepancies.
  • Lead root-cause analysis activities focused on reducing recurring claim denials, payment issues, and workflow inefficiencies.
  • Drive improvements that increase reimbursement accuracy, accelerate cash collections, and reduce outstanding accounts receivable.

Financial Controls, Reporting & Reconciliation

  • Ensure accurate and timely month-end revenue cycle reporting for health-system partner pharmacies.
  • Oversee reconciliation processes connecting pharmacy dispensing data, adjudication activity, remittance information, cash receipts, and reported accounts receivable balances.
  • Partner closely with Accounting and Finance to support financial reporting accuracy and revenue integrity.
  • Maintain controls surrounding payment posting, adjustments, write-offs, refunds, and reimbursement activity.
  • Monitor and report on key performance indicators including:
    • Cash collections
    • AR aging
    • Denial and rejection rates
    • Payment exception trends
    • Reconciliation accuracy
    • Productivity and service-level performance

Team Leadership & Operational Excellence

  • Lead, mentor, and develop revenue cycle managers, supervisors, and team members.
  • Establish performance expectations and accountability measures across the organization.
  • Align staffing strategies and operational capacity with growth objectives and service commitments.
  • Support budgeting, forecasting, workforce planning, and resource allocation efforts.
  • Foster a culture focused on accountability, collaboration, service excellence, and continuous improvement.

Process Improvement & Technology

  • Standardize specialty pharmacy revenue cycle workflows and best practices across partner pharmacy locations.
  • Lead automation and process improvement initiatives focused on reimbursement accuracy, efficiency, and scalability.
  • Partner with IT and business stakeholders on system enhancements, integrations, testing, and implementations.
  • Collaborate with pharmacy operations, patient access, and reimbursement teams to improve overall claim performance and patient outcomes.
  • Develop strong working relationships with health-system partners, payers, PBMs, and third-party vendors to resolve complex reimbursement challenges.

Compliance & Audit Readiness

  • Lead responses to PBM audits, payer audits, partner reviews, and financial audits.
  • Manage audit findings, recoupment disputes, and remediation plans.
  • Ensure compliance with organizational policies and all applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Safeguard patient and financial information while maintaining strong operational controls.

Qualifications

  • 8+ years of progressive experience in specialty pharmacy revenue cycle management, specialty pharmacy reimbursement, pharmacy financial operations, or pharmacy accounts receivable management.
  • 5+ years of leadership experience managing revenue cycle, reimbursement, billing, collections, or pharmacy operations teams.
  • Deep understanding of:
    • Specialty pharmacy reimbursement
    • Pharmacy benefit management (PBM) operations
    • Pharmacy claims adjudication
    • NCPDP claim transactions
    • Pharmacy accounts receivable management
    • Claim rejections, reversals, and rebills
    • Remittance processing and cash application
    • Pharmacy revenue integrity and reconciliation
  • Proven success improving reimbursement performance, cash collections, and operational efficiency.
  • Experience supporting financial controls, revenue reconciliation, and reporting processes.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder-management capabilities.
