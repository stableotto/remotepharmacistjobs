Pharmacy Services Specialist

RemoteMid-levelPosted Sep 01, 2026Workday

Job Description

Job Description Summary
We’re hiring a Pharmacy Services Specialist to assist in the development and management of pharmacy benefit administration for current and new plan designs or products. Provides daily coordination of activities related to operations of pharmacy benefit management or nonclinical review and call center support. Serves as subject matter expert for current processes and new initiatives.

What You’ll Do

  • Provides daily coordination of activities related to operations of pharmacy benefit management.

  • Provides support to staff and coordinates tasks regarding the research, development, implementation and evaluation of programs, projects and initiatives.

  • Serves as resource to staff and subject matter experts assigned initiatives to provide input regarding pharmacy needs, impacts and projects. 

  • Resolves issues and escalates to management as appropriate; assists management in addressing clinical and contractual issues.

  • Coordinates review, implementation, and quality assurance of benefit plan administration with internal stakeholders and PBM account team.

  • Resolves or coordinates the resolution of escalated customer service inquiries and grievances internally and with PBM, including researching and authorizing/making pharmacy claim adjustments as appropriate.

  • Coordinates the resolution of Pharmacy Coverage Determinations with Clinical Staff and/or external partners as appropriate

  • Coordinates the resolution of pharmacy claim reconsiderations and appeals with the Appeals department and/or external partners as appropriate.

  • Responsible for responding to and coordinating review and approval of RFPs, government program applications, and similar requests.

What You Bring

  • High School Diploma or GED

  • 3+ years of experience in related fields

Bonus Points (preferred qualifications)

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

  • Excellent time management and organizational abilities.

  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite

  • Must be able to work weekends and holidays as needed to support business and operational requirements.

What You’ll Get 

  • The opportunity to work at the cutting edge of health care delivery with a team that’s deeply invested in the community  

  • Work-life balance, flexibility, and the autonomy to do great work   

  • Medical, dental, and vision coverage along with numerous health and wellness programs   

  • Parental leave and support plus adoption and surrogacy assistance   

  • Career development programs and tuition reimbursement for continued education   

  • 401k match including an annual company contribution   

  • Learn more
