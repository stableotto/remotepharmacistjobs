About the Opportunity

Join MVP’s Pharmacy team and help our members get timely access to the medications they need. As a Prior Authorization Pharmacy Technician, you will process coverage requests from providers, apply clinical criteria, and work closely with our pharmacists and physicians to make sure decisions are accurate, compliant, and on time.

 

What You’ll Do 

  • Receive and review prior authorization requests submitted by fax, ePA, and phone; confirm member eligibility and that each request is complete
  • Apply approved clinical criteria to approve qualifying requests and route the rest to a pharmacist for review
  • Contact prescribers and pharmacies to gather missing information
  • Document every case accurately in our UM system and meet regulatory turnaround times
  • Answer provider and member questions about formulary status, coverage, and claim rejections
  • Support appeals, quality audits, and process-improvement work as needed
  • Other duties as assigned by leadership

  • Education, Licensures, & Certifications
