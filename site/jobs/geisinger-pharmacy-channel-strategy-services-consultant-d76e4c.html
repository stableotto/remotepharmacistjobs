Job Summary:

Responsible for supporting the development of performance and business plans for partner health systems to develop and optimize existing home infusion pharmacy services.

Job Duties:

  • Supports Senior consultant in interview facilitation, preparation of minutes, development of agenda, and tracking open items
  • Supports development of presentation deliverables across all senior members of entire Geisinger Pharmacy Team
  • Support project management, data and analytics, and deliverables to meet client needs on account
  • Supports development of all final deliverables to include both financial modeling and executive level presentations
  • 15% nation-wide travel may be required

Work is typically performed in an office environment. Accountable for satisfying all job specific obligations and complying with all organization policies and procedures. The specific statements in this profile are not intended to be all-inclusive. They represent typical elements considered necessary to successfully perform the job.

Position Details:

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Healthcare Administration, Pharmacy, Life Sciences, Public Health, Analytics, Finance, or related field.
  • Two (2) years of experience in healthcare consulting, healthcare strategy, healthcare operations, pharmacy operations, business planning, analytics, project management, or a related field.
  • Demonstrated experience analyzing data and developing reports, presentations, or recommendations for business stakeholders.
  • Strong communication, project management, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Five (5) years of experience in healthcare consulting, specialty pharmacy, infusion services, pharmacy strategy, healthcare operations, business planning, or related healthcare leadership roles.
  • Experience supporting client-facing consulting engagements, including stakeholder interviews, executive presentations, and strategic recommendations.
  • Experience developing financial models, pro formas, business cases, market assessments, and strategic recommendations.
  • Experience working with health systems, provider organizations, pharmacy service lines, specialty pharmacy, infusion services, or healthcare delivery organizations.
  • Knowledge of specialty pharmacy, home infusion, ambulatory infusion, medication access services, 340B programs, or health system pharmacy operations.
  • Master's degree (MBA, MHA, MPH, PharmD, or related field) preferred.
