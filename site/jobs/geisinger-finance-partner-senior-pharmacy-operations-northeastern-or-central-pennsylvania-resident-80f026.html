Job Summary:

The Finance Partner Senior will provide strategic and operational financial support for assigned business units and their respective leadership teams of Geisinger Health. This includes facilitating operating and capital forecasts, the long-range financial model, business plan financial models, monthly operating reviews, financial improvement initiatives, promotion of financial and operational benchmarking, and assures compliance with financial standards and procedures. This role provides leadership within the department by mentoring other Finance Partners and assisting to develop a high performing team. Additionally, this role will serve as a project manager to ensure goal achievement through collaboration and coordination with centralized finance teams and operational partners.

Job Duties:

Work from home with one day per week on-site in Danville PA.

Preferred experience: Pharmacy and/or Health Plan.

  • Leads a monthly operating review process for the business unit including variance reporting and coordinates and supports development of remediation and corrective action plans with operations leadership for all material variances to plan. 
  • Provides regular presentations of financial performance to internal (operational leadership). 
  • Supports financial acumen education for business units’ operational teams.
