Pharmacy Technology Analyst I

Entry-level · USA, TN, Chattanooga, Remote
RemoteEntry-levelPosted Oct 26, 2026Workday

We are hiring a Pharmacy Technology Analyst I to support our Pharmacy team!

 

This role sits at the intersection of pharmacy operations and technology. You will support pharmacy platforms and applications, system connectivity and access, and operational data feeds while partnering with internal teams and external vendors on enhancements, requirements, testing, and issue resolution. Your work will help ensure our technology and processes continue to meet business, compliance, and regulatory requirements.

 

To be successful, you’ll bring knowledge of pharmacy benefits combined with strong technical aptitude. Ideal candidates will have experience with requirements gathering, data mapping, and testing, along with strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. The ability to manage multiple priorities, learn new technologies, and communicate effectively with business, technical, and vendor partners will also be important.

 

Note:

  • This position may require after-hours or weekend support for production releases and major outages.
  • This is a remote, work-from-home position, but the final round of interviews will take place on-site in our Chattanooga, TN office.

 

Job Responsibilities

  • Technology Implementation: Support execution of pharmacy technology strategies to improve efficiency and align with business needs.
  • Task Execution: Perform foundational tasks such as data entry, tracking deliverables, and organizing technical materials.
  • Issue Escalation: Identify and escalate basic technical issues, learning to assess their potential business impact.
  • Initiative Coordination: Support pharmacy technology projects by documenting requirements and tasks.
  • Documentation Accuracy: Maintain version control and accuracy of technical documentation and system updates.
  • Training Participation: Engage in onboarding and technical training sessions to build familiarity with pharmacy systems and workflows.
  • Compliance Alignment: Understand how technical processes align with regulatory and policy standards.

 

Job Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience required. Equivalent work experience is defined as 4 years of professional work experience.

 

Experience

  • Minimum 3 years Healthcare Industry experience required
  • Minimum 2 years experience with operational analysis, design and understanding of integrated, interdependent, and interfaced systems and their impacts on each other.

 

Skills/Certifications

  • Ability to assist with technology implementation and task execution
  • Skill in maintaining accurate documentation and version control
  • Strength in identifying and escalating technical issues
  • Capability to support project coordination and team collaboration
  • Commitment to building technical knowledge and compliance awareness
