Pharmacist Lead

Senior · US - Remote
RemoteSeniorPosted Mar 19, 2026Workday

AnewHealth is one of the nation’s leading pharmacy care management companies that specializes in caring for people with the most complex, chronic needs—wherever they call home. We enable better outcomes for patients and the healthcare organizations who support them. Established in 2023 through the combination of ExactCare and Tabula Rasa HealthCare, we provide a suite of solutions that includes comprehensive pharmacy services; full-service pharmacy benefit management; and specialized support services for Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly. With over 1,400 team members, we care for more than 100,000 people across all 50 states.

Job Details

The Pharmacist Lead is responsible for overseeing the daily operations of a medication therapy management (MTM) pharmacy team of pharmacists and pharmacy technicians while providing high-quality MTM services. This role combines leadership and management of an MTM pharmacy team to achieve clinical service team goals while also executing clinical responsibilities, including comprehensive medication reviews (CMRs), prescriber recommendations to improve patient’s drug therapy, and addressing adherence barriers with patients who receive medications at the ExactCare Pharmacy. The Pharmacist Lead ensures compliance with all regulatory, vendor contract and internal requirements, promotes patient safety, and drives excellence in service and clinical outcomes.

Responsibilities

Leadership & Team Management

  • Develop MTM team strategy and processes to achieve clinical service team goals

  • Manage and supervise daily activities of pharmacists and pharmacy technicians

  • Schedule, train, mentor, and evaluate pharmacy team members

  • Foster a collaborative, professional, and patient-centered work environment

  • Address performance issues, workflow challenges, and staffing needs

  • Lead team meetings and support continuous improvement initiatives

  • Implement and monitor standard operating procedures (SOPs)

  • Handle escalated patient, provider, or operational issues

Clinical & Pharmacist Responsibilities

  • Assess medication appropriateness, effectiveness, safety, and adherence

  • Perform comprehensive medication reviews (CMRs), targeted medication reviews, prescriber outreach and recommendations, and address adherence barriers

  • Provide patient counseling and education on medications and disease states

  • Collaborate with prescribers and healthcare teams to optimize patient outcomes

  • Document clinical interventions and patient care activities accurately

Quality, Compliance & Performance

  • Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) related to quality, productivity and revenue

  • Ensure adherence to legal, privacy, compliance, contractual, and quality assurance standards and requirements

  • Perform quality assurance review of recorded calls and include in performance management of pharmacy team members

  • Promote operational efficiency and best practices to achieve desired outcomes

Experience

  • Previous experience managing, supervising, leading or overseeing the work of others

  • Experience in a patient care environment

  • Prior interface with at least one of the following preferred: Outcomes, MedMonitor, MTMPath, and EQUIPP

  • Prior Managed Care or MTM experience

Skills & Abilities

  • Strong leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills

  • Excellent clinical judgment and attention to detail

  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment

  • Knowledge of pharmacy regulations, compliance, and quality standards

  • Experience performing Medication Therapy Management (MTM), including the completion of comprehensive medication reviews and targeted interviews

  • Strong attention to detail and high level of organization

  • Excellent communication skills and customer service

  • Proficiency in pharmacology, therapeutics and knowledge of disease management programs for the purpose of supporting the clinical aspects of this job.

  • Passion to help people
