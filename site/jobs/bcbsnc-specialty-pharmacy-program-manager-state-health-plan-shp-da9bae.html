Job Description

The Specialty Pharmacy Program Manager, State Health Plan (SHP), supports the development and execution of SHP specialty pharmacy affordability and unit-cost initiatives. The role manages defined specialty pharmacy programs and initiatives, translating SHP priorities and contractual commitments into executable workplans and measurable results. The position partners across internal and vendor teams to identify, implement, and monitor opportunities that improve specialty drug affordability while supporting member access and appropriate care.

1. Specialty Pharmacy Program Execution and Ownership

Manage assigned SHP specialty pharmacy programs and unit-cost initiatives from opportunity assessment through implementation and performance monitoring. Develop workplans, coordinate cross-functional resources, monitor milestones and dependencies, maintain risks and issues, and drive assigned initiatives toward defined financial and operational outcomes.

2. Specialty Pharmacy Affordability and Unit-Cost Opportunities

Partner with internal stakeholders to evaluate specialty drug cost drivers and identify and implement opportunities to improve affordability.

3. Financial and Performance Measurement

Establish program baselines, expected outcomes, and ongoing performance measures. Monitor specialty drug spend, unit-cost performance, implementation results, and realized savings, and identify variances requiring intervention or escalation.

4. SHP and Cross-Functional Partnership

Translate SHP priorities and requirements into coordinated specialty pharmacy initiatives. Partner across clinical, pharmacy, contracting, network, finance, analytics, operations, compliance, and other enterprise teams to resolve dependencies and support implementation.

5. Vendor, Provider, and Program Coordination

Support specialty pharmacy vendor, provider, and partner initiatives as applicable, including implementation activities, performance monitoring, issue resolution, and service-level or contractual requirements. Escalate material performance, financial, or implementation risks through established governance channels.

6. Analysis, Recommendations, and Executive Communication

Analyze program performance and specialty pharmacy opportunities and develop clear, evidence-based recommendations. Prepare reporting and presentations that communicate financial impact, implementation status, risks, decisions needed, and program outcomes to SHP and internal leadership.

7. Continuous Improvement

Identify opportunities to improve specialty pharmacy processes, governance, measurement, and execution. Recommend and implement changes within assigned programs to improve affordability, operational effectiveness, and member outcomes.
