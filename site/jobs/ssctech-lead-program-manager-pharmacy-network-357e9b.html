Job Description

Job Title:  Lead Program Manager Pharmacy Network

Locations: 100% Remote  

Get To Know Us:

SS&C is leading the way.  We continue to look for todays and tomorrow’s brightest talent, those that embody a spirit to improve not only their lives, but those around them. From college students to seasoned and experienced professionals, we encourage you to apply. SS&C prides itself on hiring diverse, honest, dynamic individuals, who value collaboration, accountability, and innovation to name a few.  As Lead Program Manager Pharmacy Network, you will join a small, collaborative team promoting better health and financial outcomes for consumers through the integration of process oversight and improvements, industry expertise, and transformative digital capabilities.

Why You Will Love It Here!

  • Flexibility: Hybrid Work Model and Business Casual Dress Code, including jeans
  • Your Future: 401k Matching Program, Professional Development Reimbursement
  • Work/Life Balance: Flexible Personal/Vacation Time Off, Sick Leave, Paid Holidays
  • Your Wellbeing: Medical, Dental, Vision, Employee Assistance Program, Parental Leave
  • Wide Ranging Perspectives: Committed to Celebrating the Variety of Backgrounds, Talents and Experiences of Our Employees
  • Training: Hands-On, Team-Customized, including SS&C University
  • Extra Perks: Discounts on fitness clubs, travel and more!

What You Will Get To Do:

  • This position will be responsible for working with strategic partners to define, implement, and support optimization programs in support of SS&C clients
  • Lead end‑to‑end product delivery: discovery, requirements, scoping, prioritization, delivery planning, launch, adoption, and continuous improvement for pharmacy network products and services
  • Responsible for systems and processes that support pharmacy network participation and reimbursement for Commercial, Medicare Part D, Discount Card Programs, and Manufacture Coupon Programs.
  • Manage negotiations and relationships with pharmacy partners to ensure access for retail and specialty networks.
  • Provide insight for various aspects of external vendor contract negotiations, service level performance, system enhancements, system controls, and issue resolution
  • Design and maintain policy and procedures supporting overall compliance including; contract, state/regulatory requirements
  • Leverage technology expertise to engage across the enterprise to identify and implement opportunities for automation through artificial intelligence

What You Will Bring:

  • Degree in Business, Mathematics, Actuarial Science or other related area or equivalent work-related experience
  • Must have 6+ years’ experience in Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) experience
  • Must be able to understand and evaluate risk and effectively prioritize workload
  • Experience with negotiating and managing contractual guarantees
  • 5+ years’ experience with modeling and data management
  • Advanced SQL, DB Visualizer and Excel skills required
  • Experience in project/program management
  • Strong organizational and decision-making skills with a high degree of mathematical aptitude
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to interact with senior management and key business users
  • Demonstrated ability to multi-task

Thank you for your interest in SS&C! If applicable, to further explore this opportunity, please apply directly with us through our Careers page on our corporate website: www.ssctech.com/careers.
