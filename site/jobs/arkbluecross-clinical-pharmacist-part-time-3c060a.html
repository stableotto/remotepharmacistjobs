Job Summary

The Clinical Pharmacist will report to and operate under the direction of Pharmacy Leadership and will also be expected to work cooperatively and sometimes independently with all medical directors and enterprise personnel.

Requirements

EDUCATION

Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy from an accredited college of pharmacy

LICENSING/CERTIFICATION

Holds an active and current, unrestricted license in pharmacy in the state(s) where job duties are performed required

EXPERIENCE

Minimum three (3) years' pharmacy practice experience

Minimum three (3) years' experience in managed pharmacy care and drug utilization review preferred

This position may require up to or around 24 hours a week.
