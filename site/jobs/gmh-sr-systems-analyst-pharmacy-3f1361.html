Job Summary

Responsibilities:

Service Level Management

  • Deliver Tier 3 technical support with emphasis on complex specialty workflows, integrations, and advanced configurations.
  • Perform advanced troubleshooting and root cause analysis for issues beyond the scope of general analyst support.
  • Participate in on-call rotations and provide after-hours, weekend, and on-site support for go-lives, downtime events, upgrades, and drills specific to specialty systems.

Optimization, Governance & KPI Oversight

  • Mentor junior and mid-level analysts and provide technical leadership within specialty domains.
  • Participate in governance forums, project teams, and strategy discussions, providing expert guidance on specialty systems.
  • Lead KPI-driven optimization initiatives, Gold Stars programs, and integration validation efforts to improve clinical and operational outcomes.

Business Continuity, Vulnerability, & Feasibility Management

  • Ensure compliance with HIPAA, CMS, Joint Commission, and applicable regulatory and audit requirements.
  • Build, maintain, and refine Business Continuity Access (BCA) and downtime procedures for specialty platforms.
  • Identify and remediate vulnerabilities in specialty systems.
  • Serve as a liaison with operations during outages affecting specialty systems.

System Maintenance & Design Standardization

  • Lead and execute unit, integrated, and user acceptance testing for specialty modules.
  • Prepare and validate testing scripts; document outcomes; coordinate go-live activities.
  • Audit and validate specialty system builds and upgrades, ensuring compliance with integration standards and Change Control governance.

Technology & Application Expertise

  • Serve as the primary subject-matter expert for Epic specialty modules and integrated ancillary systems (e.g., Beaker, Radiant, Cupid, LIS, PACS, imaging platforms).
  • Lead the configuration, optimization, and maintenance of specialty workflows, order sets, clinical content, and advanced system features.
  • Evaluate upstream and downstream impacts across Epic and ancillary platforms using HL7, FHIR, APIs, and integrated architectures.
  • Act as the primary liaison with Epic and third-party vendors, coordinating issue escalation, solution validation, and delivery.

Qualifications:

  • Experience in enterprise applications and IT infrastructure
  • 3+ years of related job experience.
  • PharmD

While this position is remote, we’re only able to hire candidates who reside in Georgia, Texas, Tennessee, North Carolina, Florida, South Carolina, Michigan, or Colorado.
