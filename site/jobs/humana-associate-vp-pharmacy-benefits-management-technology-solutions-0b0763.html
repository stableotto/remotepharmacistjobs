The AVP, PBM Technology Solutions devises an effective strategy for executing and delivering on IT business initiatives. The AVP, PBM Technology Solutions requires an in-depth understanding of how organization capabilities interrelate across segments and/or enterprise-wide.

Role Summary

The AVP, PBM Technology Solutions is responsible for leading the strategy, architecture, and delivery of technology solutions that enable PBM business growth and operational excellence. This role combines executive leadership with hands-on technical engagement, including architecture oversight, solution design, vendor management, and evaluation of emerging technologies to ensure scalable, resilient, and future-ready platforms.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Leadership & Transformation

  • Define and execute a multi-year technology strategy aligned with business priorities

  • Lead large-scale modernization and transformation initiatives

  • Balance short-term delivery with long-term platform evolution

Hands-On Technical Leadership & Architecture

  • Provide hands-on technical leadership, actively guiding design decisions, architecture reviews, and critical solution trade-offs

  • Partner with Enterprise Architecture and Solution Architecture teams to define and govern target state architecture across application, integration, data, and cloud platforms

  • Collaborate with vendor partners on architecture, solution delivery, governance and technical resiliency

  • Lead architecture forums and ensure alignment with enterprise standards

  • Drive engineering excellence through scalability, reusability, and performance optimization

Technology Evaluation & Innovation

  • Evaluate emerging technologies, platforms, and tools

  • Lead technology selection decisions including build vs buy

  • Drive adoption of AI, automation, and advanced analytics

  • Continuously modernize the technology stack

Technology Delivery & Platform Ownership

  • Own end-to-end delivery and lifecycle of enterprise platforms

  • Ensure systems are scalable, secure, and resilient

  • Drive outcome-based delivery across teams

Financial & Operational Accountability

  • Manage IT spend across labor, vendors, and platforms

  • Optimize cost and delivery efficiency

Leadership & Talent Development

  • Lead high-performing engineering and architecture teams

  • Develop strong technical leadership bench

  • Foster a culture of innovation and accountability

Stakeholder & Vendor Management

  • Partner with business and enterprise leaders

  • Communicate effectively with senior stakeholders

  • Manage strategic vendor relationships
