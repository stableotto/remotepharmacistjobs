Clinical Formulary Pharmacist

Mid-level · Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Nov 19, 2026Workday

Reporting to the Manager of Clinical Pharmacy, the Clinical Formulary Pharmacist is primarily responsible for supporting development and maintenance activities for medical drug formulary for ASO, Medicare, and any future commercial lines of business as well as any pharmacy drug formulary development and benefit design needs with our contracted pharmacy benefit manager for all lines of business. The responsibilities of this position include working with the Manager of Clinical Pharmacy to monitor and analyze drug trends, new drug releases, and review primary literature that will ultimately drive pharmacy and medical drug formulary as well as the execution of necessary formulary work in compliance with accreditation and regulatory standards. In addition, this role will work collaboratively with our Medical Director and Utilization Management teams to develop complete and up-to-date utilization management criteria and to review any specialty medication requiring a specialty consult as defined by our formulary team.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:

EDUCATION, CERTIFICATION, AND/OR LICENSURE:

1. Must possess current and unrestricted license to practice pharmacy issued by state of residence. 

EXPERIENCE:

1. Four (4) years of experience as a pharmacist in a clinical setting

2. Two (2) years of formulary or managed care experience

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

EDUCATION, CERTIFICATION, AND/OR LICENSURE:

1. Specific knowledge of pharmacotherapeutics and drug study analysis

2. Experience in strategy, design and management of formulary preferred

3. Specific knowledge of pharmacoeconomics and drug impact analysis preferred

4. Specific knowledge of CMS regulations, HIPAA and regulatory environment preferred

CORE DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: The statements described here are intended to describe the general nature of work being performed by people assigned to this position.  They are not intended to be constructed as an all-inclusive list of all responsibilities and duties.  Other duties may be assigned.

1. Creation of drug monographs and reviews of new drug entities to market in a timely manner as defined by accreditation or regulatory standards per line of business as well as formulary recommendations for all new drug formulations and indication or safety updates.

2. Update existing criteria and make recommendations regarding drug inclusion and utilization as new drug information becomes available.

3. Own the operational execution of all clinical and formulary decisions including working with operations team  to ensure all formulary changes are implemented and maintained.

4. Own the communication needs for all clinical and formulary decision changes including website and criteria postings.

5. Drive pharmacy formulary strategy implementation by working with leadership to monitor and review drug trends, examine utilization management, and competitor strategies.

6. Ensure all drug policies and criteria are reviewed at least annually and remain accurate with the most up-to-date information.

7. Present and participate in discussion of medical literature review for evaluation of place in therapy for new drugs or disease management concepts in regular clinical policy discussion meetings with our Medical Directors and leadership.

8. Prepare all presentation materials for regularly scheduled P&T committee meetings timely and ensure documents are distributed to participants in advance of each meeting.

9. Present and actively participate in discussion of formulary recommendations made during regularly scheduled P&T committee meetings.

10. Work with Medical Directors and UM teams to review and make recommendations on specialty drugs requiring a specialty consult as defined by our organization.

11. Work with the Manager of Clinical Pharmacy to resolve questions and disputes from our clients and participating providers regarding formulary decisions.

12. Assist in the design and development of tools and mechanisms to document the outcomes and value associated with pharmacoeconomic activities that drive formulary and plan benefit design strategy.

13. Assist in development of scheduled and ad hoc reports and analyses related to pharmaceutical value-based efforts in collaboration with data analysts to identify and implement opportunities for formulary decisions to reduce cost and increase market competitiveness.

14. Monitor and maintain knowledge of pipeline and biosimilar drugs in the marketplace to assess how these may drive formulary strategy.

15. Assist in monthly analysis of claims data to assess effectiveness of current formulary strategies.

16. Establish and foster interdepartmental relationships to ensure all formulary and benefit design strategy is being implemented and serve as a point of contact when needed for clarification and questions.

17. Serve as a clinical pharmacy preceptor for residents and rotational pharmacy students as needed.

18. Assist in creation of training materials for formulary processes and maintenance.

19. Assist in creation of clinical development tools for education as it relates to formulary decisions and maintenance.
