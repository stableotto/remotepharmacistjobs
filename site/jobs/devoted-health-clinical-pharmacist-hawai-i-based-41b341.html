Job Description

Location: Hawaiʻi (Remote/Hybrid - Must reside in the islands) 

Licensure: State of Hawaiʻi Pharmacist License (Required)

About the Role: Serving Our Local Communities

This is a unique opportunity for a motivated pharmacist to join a high-performing, award-winning team at the forefront of pharmacy innovation. We are looking for a clinician who understands the unique healthcare landscape of Hawaiʻi and is passionate about providing patient-centered care that honors our local culture and community.

You will play a vital role in ensuring our members receive the right medications at the right time. By focusing on medication adherence and chronic disease education, you will drive better clinical outcomes for our neighbors and ‘ohana. You will also serve as a bridge between members and their providers, navigating the complexities of medication therapy management with empathy and expertise.

Required Skills & Experience

  • Local Residency: Must currently reside in the State of Hawaiʻi.

  • Licensure: Active Pharmacist License in the State of Hawaiʻi, in good standing, at the time of application.

  • Clinical Experience: 3+ years of clinical experience in direct patient care.

  • Communication: Ability to communicate complex medical information with a "talk story" warmth that builds trust with local patients.

Desired Skills & Experience

  • Experience working with collaborative pharmacy practice agreements.

  • Board certification or specialized experience in complex geriatric care (Kupuna care).

  • Multilingual skills (e.g., Tagalog, Ilocano, Cantonese, or Hawaiian) are a significant plus.

Your Responsibilities & Impact

Pharmacist Consult Services

  • Work directly with patients to manage chronic conditions through personalized care plans.

  • Develop and provide educational materials tailored to the specific needs of our local population.

  • Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to iterate on disease management processes.

  • Help members and providers navigate their benefits to ensure healthcare is accessible and affordable.

Medication Therapy Management (MTM)

  • Conduct comprehensive medication reviews for members with complex regimens.

  • Identify and resolve drug therapy issues to optimize care and improve health outcomes.

Quality & Compassionate Care

  • Drive "5-Star" performance on pharmacy-related clinical measures through best-in-class care.

  • Address therapy gaps (e.g., statin initiation for diabetes or cardiovascular health).

  • Interact with members with genuine compassion, helping them reach their individual health goals.

Practice Operations

  • Support on-call rotations for urgent pharmacy needs.

  • Act as a clinical subject matter expert for our pharmacy model development.

  • Mentor and train the wider team on pharmacotherapy and practice-related topics.
