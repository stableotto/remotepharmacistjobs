Job Description: Senior Epic General Pharmacy Billing Consultant

Position Type: Contract (W2)
Certification Requirement: Epic Certified (required)

Key Responsibilities

  • Participate in initiative-related meetings to represent revenue cycle interests and provide subject matter expertise
  • Identify and communicate downstream impacts of pharmacy build decisions on Hospital Billing (HB), Professional Billing (PB), and Claims teams
  • Provide build guidance, advising on how proposed builds affect billing workflows and revenue cycle outcomes
  • Collaborate closely with billing team members to support and inform build efforts
  • Support the expansion of compound medication offerings within the Epic pharmacy build

Required Qualifications

  • Epic certification (required)
  • Demonstrated experience across all HB, PB, and Claims modules
  • Strong background in Epic build, with the ability to translate build decisions into revenue cycle impact assessments
  • Proven experience as a revenue cycle consultant in a healthcare/Epic environment

Required Certifications

  • Epic HB Certification
  • Epic HB Claims Certification
  • Epic PB Certification

Ideal Candidate Profile

A well-rounded revenue cycle professional who is comfortable working cross-functionally, can speak knowledgeably to both technical build considerations and downstream billing/claims implications, and has direct experience supporting pharmacy-related Epic builds.
