AnewHealth is one of the nation’s leading pharmacy care management companies that specializes in caring for people with the most complex, chronic needs—wherever they call home. We enable better outcomes for patients and the healthcare organizations who support them. Established in 2023 through the combination of ExactCare and Tabula Rasa HealthCare, we provide a suite of solutions that includes comprehensive pharmacy services; full-service pharmacy benefit management; and specialized support services for Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly. With over 1,400 team members, we care for more than 100,000 people across all 50 states.

Job Details

ExactCare is seeking PRN Pharmacists that have the ability to take on a fluctuating schedule that is dictated by the needs of the business. Candidates must be comfortable with no set guaranteed hours and the potential of some months with no hours.

PRN Pharmacists are expected to deliver on quality standards that allow ExactCare to ensure proper medication profiles are managed, reviewed and verified. Additionally, the PRN Pharmacist is responsible for the proper dispensing of all medications filled at ExactCare to ensure the correct and safe supply of medications are delivered to each patient.

Our PRN Pharmacists may also be considered a ‘Clinical Pharmacist’, meaning you will have the opportunity to perform comprehensive medication reviews and targeted interviews. Therefore, clinical knowledge of current industry standards is vital. The PRN Pharmacist is responsible for adhering to company policies and procedures.  

Responsibilities

  • Ensuring clinical accuracy and execution of patient prescriptions

  • Verification of hard copy and digital Rx’s

  • Communication with healthcare professionals and making sure that the Dr. call line is answered

  • Supervision and execution of incoming and outgoing prescription transfers

  • Perform DUR to identify MTM eligible patients and identify clinical intervention per patient with follow up

  • Ensure all legal, privacy and compliance requirements are met

  • Other duties as appropriate

Skills & Abilities

  • Team oriented

  • Exceptional standards of customer care

  • Strong attention to detail and high level of organization

  • Problem solving ability

  • Ability to multi-task

  • Excellent communication skills and customer service

  • Self-starter with little to no supervision needed

  • Passion to help people

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or Doctorate in Pharmacy (PharmD)

  • Active Pharmacist license in good standing

  • Ability to take on a fluctuating schedule dictated by the needs of the business

  • Pharmacist dispensing and verification experience

  • Clinical judgment experience

  • Strong work ethic and ability to collaborate with multiple partn
