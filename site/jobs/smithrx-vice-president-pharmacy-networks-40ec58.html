Job Summary:

The Vice President of Pharmacy Networks is a critical executive leadership role responsible for the strategic vision, contracting, and end-to-end management of SmithRx’s national pharmacy network. You will lead negotiations and foster relationships with retail chains, independent pharmacies, Pharmacy Services Administration Organizations (PSAOs), specialty pharmacies, and mail-order facilities.

In this role, you will ensure our network aligns with our transparent, pass-through pricing model while maintaining exceptional coverage, quality, and cost-efficiency for our clients and members. You will oversee a team dedicated to network development, compliance, credentialing, and performance optimization.

What you will do:

Network Strategy & Contracting

  • Lead Network Strategy: Design and execute the overarching strategy for SmithRx’s national and custom pharmacy networks, ensuring alignment with our transparent business model and client needs.
  • Contract Negotiation: Lead high-level negotiations with major retail chains, PSAOs, specialty pharmacies, and mail-order partners to secure competitive reimbursement rates and favorable contract terms without relying on spread pricing.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Identify and develop innovative network programs (e.g., high-performance or narrow networks, direct-to-consumer delivery models) to drive down drug costs and improve member access.

Network Management & Operations

  • Oversee Network Performance: Monitor and manage the ongoing performance of the pharmacy network against defined SLAs, ensuring adherence to contractual terms, quality standards, and pricing accuracy.
  • Credentialing & Compliance: Direct the credentialing and re-credentialing processes, ensuring all network pharmacies comply with federal, state, and SmithRx regulatory standards.
  • Audit & Fraud Prevention: Oversee the pharmacy audit team to manage network integrity, minimize Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA), and execute audit recoveries effectively.
  • Government programs readiness: Implementation of needed policies and procedures to meet CMS oversight requirements. 

Cross-Functional Leadership

    • Internal Alignment: Partner closely with Sales, Customer Success, Clinical Strategy, Pricing/Underwriting, and Product teams to ensure network capabilities are accurately represented in the market and successfully integrated into our technology platforms.
    • Data & Analytics: Collaborate with data and analytics teams to monitor network profitability, utilization trends, and cost models to proactively identify areas for financial optimization.
    • Team Development: Build, mentor, and scale a high-performing team of network managers, contracting specialists, and analysts. Foster a culture of accountability, integrity, and courage

What you will bring to SmithRx:

  • Experience: 12+ years of progressive leadership experience in pharmacy network management, pharmacy contracting, or PBM operations.
  • Experience across lines of business: Commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, Exchange 
  • Industry Expertise: Deep understanding of the PBM ecosystem, pharmacy reimbursement models (AWP, MAC, NADAC), and the operational differences between traditional and pass-through/transparent PBMs.
  • Negotiation Skills: Proven track record of successfully negotiating complex, multi-million dollar contracts with major national pharmacy chains and PSAOs.
  • Leadership: Demonstrated experience building, leading, and mentoring cross-functional and remote teams in a high-growth environment.
  • Analytical Acumen: Strong financial and analytical skills with the ability to understand complex pricing models and translate data into actionable network strategies.
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Business, Healthcare Administration, Finance, or a related field; PharmD or Master’s degree (MBA/MHA) is highly preferred.
