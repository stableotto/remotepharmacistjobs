Pharmacy Quality Coordinator

Mid-level · $23.23 - $39.61/hr · Remote-MS
RemoteMid-levelPosted Nov 06, 2026Workday

Position Purpose:
Primary liaison between internal and external (e.g. Providers and pharmacies) teams to advance pharmacy quality (Part D Patient Safety Measures, HEDIS, PQA, etc.) performance. This position will own a territory and will work closely with strategist(s), trainer(s) pharmacy quality performance manager(s), and health plan partners. Through consistent relationship building, critical thinking and problem solving, this role will strengthen internal/external collaboration success.


Key Details: This is a field-based position with a Monday–Friday schedule of 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Central Time. Candidates must reside in Mississippi and be comfortable traveling up to 50%, including travel of up to 100 miles one way, to support members within the assigned area.


  • Develop and execute a comprehensive territory external (e.g. provider and pharmacy) engagement plan

  • Conduct consistent field visits with external partners to advance operational models' improving pharmacy quality performance

  • Collaborate across stakeholders to ensure critical and timely insights that promote external partner's adoption of new concepts and changes

  • Accountable for covered external partners' pharmacy quality (Part D Patient Safety Measures, HEDIS, PQA, etc.) performance

  • Executive summaries of successes and challenges in external engagement efforts; proactively solutioning or working cross-colaboratively for resolution and associated tracking

  • Ability to travel 40% of time

  • Performs other duties as assigned.

  • Complies with all policies and standards.

Education/Experience:

High School Diploma or GED required

3+ years of pharmacy experience, preferably in a managed care environment required

2+ years experience in business to business (B2B) required

1+ years experience with Medicare and/or Medicaid preferred


1+ years experience with analysis tools or systems (ex: web based, custom, proprietary, etc.) preferred

1+ years experience in the implementation of quality process improvement efforts. *Experience documenting requirements, creating training materials and working directly with end users preferred

Licenses/Certifications:
LPT - Licensed Pharmacy Tech preferred

Pay Range: $23.23 - $39.61 per hour
