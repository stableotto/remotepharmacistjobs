Pharmacist (Remote) - Part Time, Weekend Shifts

Mid-level · $58.00 - $60.00/hr · Remote

Founded in San Francisco in 2011, The Apothecarium began as a patient-focused medical dispensary and has grown into a trusted, award-winning cannabis retail brand with locations across California, Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New Jersey.

We’re known for our modern, welcoming spaces, emphasis on education, and unmatched customer service. Whether you're new to cannabis or a seasoned consumer, our highly trained cannabis associates are here to provide personalized, one-on-one consultations to help you find the products that best meet your needs.

We don’t just sell cannabis — we create experiences that promote wellness, build trust, and enrich lives. Come see why so many patients and customers choose The Apothecarium as their go-to destination for cannabis.

The Cannabis Dispensary Pharmacist is a key member of our dispensary team, responsible for ensuring the safe and compliant dispensing of medical cannabis to patients in accordance with Pennsylvania’s Medical Marijuana Program. This position involves providing expert guidance on cannabis therapies, verifying physician certifications, and ensuring accurate patient records. The pharmacist will lead patient consultations, educating them on the therapeutic use of medical cannabis, and recommend appropriate forms of consumption based on individual medical needs. The role also includes maintaining compliance with state regulations and collaborating with dispensary staff to optimize patient care and dispensary operations.

Responsibilities:

  • Conduct one-on-one patient consultations to provide education on medical cannabis, discuss consumption methods, and recommend appropriate treatments based on physician certifications.
  • Maintain accurate and confidential patient records, documenting consultations, recommendations, and any relevant information while adhering to privacy and data protection regulations.
  • Ensure strict compliance with Pennsylvania Medical Marijuana laws and dispensary regulations, including maintaining accurate records, verifying patient certifications, and managing the Prescription Monitoring Program.
  • Dispense medical cannabis products according to physician recommendations and patient needs, ensuring proper use and dosage.
  • Stay updated on the latest research in cannabis pharmacology, contributing to patient care with evidence-based recommendations and industry developments.
  • Collaborate with the Dispensary General Managers and other team members to ensure smooth operations and achieve key performance metrics.

Experience & Requirements:

  • Valid Pennsylvania Pharmacist license, in good standing.
  • Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy or PharmD.
  • 1+ years experience working in a pharmacy setting assisting patients/customers.
  • Satisfactory completion of any State-required Medical Cannabis education programs.
  • Ability to learn and understand cannabinoids and how they interact with the human body.
  • Ability to work effectively with employees, management, patients, governmental agencies, community members, and the general public.
  • Understanding of applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations as they pertain to the industry, personnel law, safety regulations, local municipal codes, and organizational rules, regulations, directives, and standard operating procedures.
  • Extensive knowledge of cannabis pharmacology and its therapeutic applications, or willingness to acquire this expertise.
  • Excellent communication, patient education skills, and customer service skills.
  • Strong attention to detail, organizational, and leadership skills.
