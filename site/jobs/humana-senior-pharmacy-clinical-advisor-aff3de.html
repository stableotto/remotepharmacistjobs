Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor

Senior · $86,300 - $118,700/yr · Remote Nationwide
RemoteSeniorPosted Jul 01, 2026Workday

Become a part of our caring community
 

The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor advises and collaborates with cross functional leaders with goals of combining clinical pharmacy acumen with holistic desired health outcomes. The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor work assignments involve moderately complex to complex issues where the analysis of situations or data requires an in-depth evaluation of variable factors.

The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor will be a part of a collaborative Pharmacy Stars team which is accountable for Humana’s Stars patient safety measure performance. The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor will develop, implement, and manage clinical program strategies to improve Medicare members' appropriate medication use with specific focus on provider and member engagement solutions delivered through multiple channels. The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor exercises independent judgment and decision making on complex issues regarding job duties and related tasks, analyzes variable factors to determine the best course of action, and works across multiple departments to execute strategies. The Senior Pharmacy Clinical Advisor influences department’s strategy and performs without direction.


Use your skills to make an impact
 

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent
  • 5+ years of healthcare or project management experience
  • Strong analytical skills
  • Skilled in using data to identify areas of concern and drive operational changes
  • Successful track record in facilitating and consulting across teams and managing projects
  • Demonstrated ability to implement projects/campaigns and achieve business goals
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Outlook, Excel and PowerPoint
  • Must be passionate about contributing to an organization focused on continuously improving consumer experiences


Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's Degree in Business Administration, Health Administration or a related field
  • Prior managed care experience
  • Understanding of metrics, trends, and the ability to analyze and identify gaps in care
  • Proven organizational and prioritization skills and ability to collaborate with multiple departments a plus
  • Understanding of CMS Stars and performance measure knowledge and experience a plus
  • Background working in quality improvements
