Product Marketing, Pharmacy (CONTRACT)

Mid-level · $75.00 - $80.00/hr · US - Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Apr 03, 2026Greenhouse

Transcarent is looking for an experienced product marketer to join our team on a contract basis and own pharmacy benefits go-to-market for a critical engagement. This is a high-impact, execution-focused role for someone who knows the PBM space deeply and can get to work fast. 

You will be the primary product marketing resource for Transcarent's pharmacy solutions — including our transparent PBM offering and pharmacy-led navigation — working closely with Product, Sales, and Consultant Relations to sharpen our story, arm the field, and support active commercial priorities. 

This is a full-time contract role. We are not looking for someone who needs a long runway to get up to speed. The is a high visibility role and the right person will be immediately credible with buyers and consultants, have a strong command of the PBM and pharmacy benefits landscape, and be able to produce high-quality work with minimal hand-holding. 

What You Will Work On 

  • Positioning and messaging Refine and maintain the core positioning for our pharmacy solutions, including our transparent PBM and pharmacy-led navigation offerings. Ensure our story is sharp and differentiated across employer and consultant audiences. 
  • Sales enablement Build and update the assets our Sales and Solutions teams use in active deals: pitch narratives, talk tracks, objection handling, competitive positioning, and proof points. Quality and field adoption matter more than volume. 
  • Consultant-facing materials Develop materials designed for benefits consultants and advisors — how we fit into the benefits stack, how we compare to traditional and transparent PBM arrangements, and what outcomes employers should expect. These need to meet consultants where they evaluate solutions, not just where we want to be. 
  • Launch support Provide go-to-market support for pharmacy product launches and updates, including messaging, tiering, and channel execution in partnership with cross-functional teammates. 

What We Are Looking For 

  • 6+ years of product marketing experience, with meaningful time spent in pharmacy benefits required 
  • Direct familiarity with how self-insured employers and benefits consultants evaluate and buy pharmacy solutions 
  • Ability to produce polished, audience-ready deliverables independently — you write well and know what good looks like 
  • Comfortable bringing structure to your environment; this role requires good judgment on prioritization and a bias toward action 
  • Experience in B2B or B2B2C healthcare; benefits navigation or health plan experience is a plus 
As a remote position, the salary range for this role is:
$75—$80 USD
