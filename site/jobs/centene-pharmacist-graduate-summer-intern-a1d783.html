Pharmacist Graduate Summer Intern

$21.00 - $30.00/hr · Remote-MO
RemotePosted Nov 06, 2026Workday

Candidates need to be located in the greater St. Louis Area!

Position Purpose: The Student Pharmacist Intern will gain hands-on experience in specialty pharmacy while working under the supervision of licensed pharmacists. This role provides exposure to the full specialty pharmacy process, including prescription intake, medication access, fulfillment, patient support, quality assurance, and pharmacy operations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support daily specialty pharmacy workflows and patient-related activities within authorized scope of practice.
  • Gain exposure to medication access, prior authorizations, financial assistance, adherence, REMS, and complex therapies.
  • Identify and help resolve workflow barriers that may delay prescription fulfillment.
  • Research pharmacy-related topics and present findings to pharmacists and pharmacy leadership.
  • Collect and analyze operational data to identify trends and process-improvement opportunities.
  • Support quality, patient safety, workflow documentation, and operational improvement initiatives.
  • Complete an internship project and present findings and recommendations to pharmacy leadership.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled and in good academic standing in an accredited Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) program.
  • Holds or is eligible to obtain required pharmacy intern registration/licensure for the applicable state.
  • Strong communication, organization, critical-thinking, and problem-solving skills.
  • Comfortable working with operational data and Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Teams.
  • Interest in specialty pharmacy, patient care, medication access, and continuous improvement.

What You’ll Gain

Real-world specialty pharmacy experience beyond the classroom, exposure to cross-functional pharmacy operations, and the opportunity to build skills that can support future career opportunities within specialty pharmacy.


Learn about various processes and functions within the Managed Care industry and develop professionally by contributing to projects that support the business.

  • Learn various job functions within the Managed Care industry and explore various career opportunities

  • Apply academic knowledge and learn new skills by contributing to various projects

  • Communicate project status and results to staff mentors and management

  • Research various legal, regulatory, and other topics within functional area and industry

  • Attend training and development presentations to enhance professional competencies
  • Performs other duties as assigned

  • Complies with all policies and standards

The pay range is: $21.00 - $30.00 per hour


Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without the need for employment-based visa sponsorship now or in the future. Sponsorship and future sponsorship are not available for this opportunity, including employment-based visa types H-1B, L-1, O-1, H-1B1, F-1, J-1, OPT, or CPT.

Education/Experience: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent undergraduate coursework completed. Must be enrolled in a graduate program at an accredited university or college in a field related to the hiring department through the internship period.
