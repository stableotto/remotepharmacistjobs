Pharmacy Trainer, Fulfillment

Mid-level · OH - Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Mar 20, 2026Workday

AnewHealth is one of the nation’s leading pharmacy care management companies that specializes in caring for people with the most complex, chronic needs—wherever they call home. We enable better outcomes for patients and the healthcare organizations who support them. Established in 2023 through the combination of ExactCare and Tabula Rasa HealthCare, we provide a suite of solutions that includes comprehensive pharmacy services; full-service pharmacy benefit management; and specialized support services for Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly. With over 1,400 team members, we care for more than 100,000 people across all 50 states.

Job Details

As a Corporate Trainer, you will play a pivotal role in enhancing workplace performance and fostering employee development across AnewHealth Fulfillment Centers. You will design and implement training programs tailored to diverse learning styles, manage key aspects of onboarding and curriculum development, and lead initiatives to address organizational skill gaps. This role combines creativity, strategic planning, and collaboration to support our workforce's growth and align training programs with company values.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate training sessions ranging from short workshops to comprehensive multi-day programs

  • Collaborate with key stakeholders across Fulfillment Centers to design technical training lessons, job aids, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and onboarding guides

  • Create high-quality training materials, such as handouts, manuals, and e-learning modules, adapted to on-site learners and site-specific workflows

  • Ensure training materials are clear, concise, and aligned with the company brand

  • Continuously evaluate and refine training programs to enhance effectiveness and sustainability

  • Conduct organization-wide assessments to identify skill and knowledge gaps

  • Lead enhanced orientation programs for new employees, offering mentorship and personalized learning support

  • Partner with cross-functional teams to brainstorm and set course goals

The above essential functions are representative of major duties of positions in this job classification. Specific duties and responsibilities may vary based upon departmental needs. Other duties may be assigned similar to the above consistent with knowledge, skills and abilities required for the job.  Not all of the duties may be assigned to a position.

Qualifications:  These represent the desired qualifications of the ideal candidate. They are not meant to limit consideration for candidates who do not meet all of the standards listed. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

Education

  • High School Diploma or equivalent GED.

Experience

  • 2+ years of Pharmacy and/or Fulfillment experience, including familiarity with systems and technologies in use

  • Proven experience as a Corporate Trainer or similar role, with experience delivering remote and hybrid training sessions

  • Preferred:  Leadership experience in developing training roles or department-specific training initiatives

Skills & Abilities

  • Strong instructional design skills, including the ability to make digital learning interactive and engaging

  • Familiarity with Pharmacy Technician licensing requirements and renewal processes

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to convert complex technical information into clear, compelling content

  • Strong organizational skills, including the ability to prioritize activities and deliverables in a high-growth, fast-paced environment

  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Teams

  • Willingness to learn and apply cross-COE knowledge

Physical/Mental Demands: This position is administrative in nature and will present physical demands requisite to a position requiring: hearing, seeing, sitting, standing, talking, and walking. The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this job. May be necessary to work extended hours as needed.

Schedule:  This is a full-time position with an expectation to work an average of 40 hours per week and be available outside of normal business hours to meet customer expectations on an ad-hoc basis. Schedules are set to accommodate the requirements of the position and the needs of the organization and may be adjusted as needed.

Location: Preferred geographic proximity to an AnewHealth Fulfillment Center with ability to travel to business site as needed. Fulfillment center locations include

  • Corona, California

  • Valley View, OH

  • Moorestown, NJ

  • Lewisville, TX

 

Travel: Travel during the first year may be up to 20% to support the successful launch of fulfillment center onboarding programs.  Additional travel may be required for special organization or department events, department team meetings, trade shows, conferences, and other client engagements.

Medical, dental and vision coverage are effective 1st of the month following date of hire.
