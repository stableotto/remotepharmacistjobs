Manager, Pharmacy Technicians

Senior · $70,100 - $126,200/yr · Remote-FL
RemoteSeniorPosted Sep 18, 2026Workday

Position Purpose: Manage the triage process of prior authorizations, including supervising pharmacy technicians, manage departmental work flow; identify and develop prior authorization procedures and policies.

Key Details: Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without the need for employment-based visa sponsorship now or in the future. Sponsorship and future sponsorship are not available for this opportunity, including employment-based visa types H-1B, L-1, O-1, H-1B1, F-1, J-1, OPT, or CPT.

  • Coordinate with various departments to research and resolve escalated complaints and inquiries
  • Monitor prior authorization processes and recommend and modify current policies and procedures
  • Ensure operational integrity, including compliance with all associated policies and procedures
  • Monitor prior authorization activities and determine appropriate staffing levels
  • Oversee department performance and review reports and audit findings to ensure optimal performance is achieved
  • Ensure provider notification letters are communicated accurately and in a timely manner
  • Oversee quality control checks and provide recommendations for improvement
  • Communicate with providers and pharmacies to advise clinical outcome of prior authorization request
  • Oversee multiple pharmacy systems to track and triage prior authorization requests for Medicaid and coverage determination requests for Medicare D recipients and determine if a pharmacist review is required

Education/Experience: Associates degree in related field or equivalent experience. 4+ years of pharmacy technician experience in a mail order prescription facility, hospital pharmacy or retail pharmacy setting. 1+ years of combined lead or supervisory and customer service experience.

License/Certification: Current state’s Pharmacy Technician license and National Certification from Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (CPhT) preferred.

 

Pay Range: $70,100.00 - $126,200.00 per year
