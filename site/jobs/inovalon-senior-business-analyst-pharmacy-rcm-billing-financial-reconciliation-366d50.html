Location: This position is designated as remote. However, employees who reside within a 40-mile radius of one of our office locations in Tampa, FL; Canonsburg, PA; or Bowie, MD will follow a hybrid work schedule and are expected to work in the office two days per week (Tuesdays and Wednesdays).

Overview

We are seeking a highly experienced Senior Business Analyst with deep expertise in specialty/infusion pharmacy, medical billing, and financial reconciliation to support enterprise SaaS implementations. This role serves as a critical liaison between clients, operations, product, and engineering teams to define business requirements, design future-state workflows, and ensure successful delivery of Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) solutions.

The ideal candidate brings strong domain knowledge in pharmacy billing, claims adjudication, payment posting, and reconciliation, and plays a key role across the full client lifecycle—from pre-sales through implementation and post-go-live optimization.

Key Responsibilities

Delivery & Strategic Execution

  • Lead complex and enterprise-wide implementations with multiple integrated workstreams
  • Own RCM functional track deliverables, ensuring alignment across stakeholders
  • Define governance standards, implementation plans, and risk mitigation strategies
  • Drive future-state workflow design across pharmacy operations and revenue cycle processes
  • Manage release coordination, dependencies, and cross-functional execution

Pharmacy Billing & Revenue Cycle Expertise

  • Serve as Subject Matter Expert (SME) in:
    • Medical and pharmacy billing (specialty and infusion)
    • Claims lifecycle management (submission, adjudication, reversals, rebilling)
    • Payment posting, ERA (835), and financial reconciliation
  • Optimize workflows to improve:
    • Clean claim rate and first-pass yield
    • Denial management and resolution
    • Days in Accounts Receivable (A/R)
    • Net collections and revenue leakage prevention
  • Analyze and resolve billing discrepancies including underpayments, overpayments, and unapplied cash
  • Ensure alignment between pharmacy systems, billing platforms, clearinghouses, and financial systems

Client Engagement & Product Collaboration

  • Work closely with clients to gather and define RCM and operational requirements
  • Translate client needs into business and system requirements for product development
  • Provide business case justification for product enhancements
  • Participate across the full lifecycle:
    • Request for Proposal (RFP )esponses and product demos
    • Implementation, testing, and go-live
    • Post-production support and optimization
  • Partner with Product Managers on roadmap and knowledge sharing

Requirements & Process Analysis

  • Elicit, document, and manage business and system requirements (BRDs, design documents)
  • Develop process workflows using tools such as Visio
  • Analyze current systems and identify improvement opportunities
  • Assess impacts of proposed solutions across systems, workflows, and stakeholders

Technical & Data Capability

  • Work within SaaS environments utilizing Agile/Scrum and full SDLC practices
  • Support system configuration and ensure alignment with business requirements
  • Define data requirements and support reporting and reconciliation processes
  • Utilize tools such as SQL and Crystal Reports for data validation and analysis
  • Support integrations across APIs, FHIR/HL7, claims (NCPDP/837), and remittance (835)

Validation & Quality Assurance

  • Lead User Acceptance Testing (UAT) strategy, planning, and execution
  • Participate in system validation prior to release
  • Triage and resolve escalated defects in collaboration with QA and engineering
  • Ensure delivered solutions meet client, operational, and financial expectations

Stakeholder & Leadership Responsibilities

  • Act as a trusted advisor to clients and internal leadership
  • Lead cross-functional discussions and align business and technical teams
  • Support training initiatives and knowledge transfer
  • Mentor junior analysts and promote BA best practices

Operational Excellence & Business Impact

  • Drive improvements in onboarding, billing accuracy, and operational efficiency
  • Support standardization, scalability, and adoption of solutions

Compliance & Governance

  • Ensure adherence to HIPAA, DEA, State Board, and regulatory requirements
  • Maintain compliance with Inovalon policies and procedures
  • Support audit readiness and financial accuracy

Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Healthcare, IT, Business, or related field (or equivalent experience)

Experience

  • 5+ years of Business Analysis experience in healthcare or SaaS
  • 2+ years of experience in medical billing and financial reconciliation
  • Experience in specialty or infusion pharmacy environments preferred
  • Strong background in Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)

Working Conditions

  • Primarily sedentary role involving extended periods of sitting and computer use
  • Occasional lifting (up to 10 pounds)
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
  • Travel for this position will include up to 10%
