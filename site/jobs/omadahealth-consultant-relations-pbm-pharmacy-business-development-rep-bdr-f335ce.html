Consultant Relations, PBM/Pharmacy Business Development Rep (BDR)

Mid-level · $190,640 - $211,970/yr · Remote, USA
RemoteMid-levelPosted Nov 24, 2026Greenhouse

Omada Health is on a mission to bend the curve of chronic disease.

Job overview:

As a member of Omada’s Consultant Relations Commercial Channel Growth team and Omada’s GLP-1 Business Unit, the Consultant Relations Business Development Representative, PBM & Pharmacy (CR BDR – PBM) is a quota-carrying, consultant-facing sales support role focused on expanding Omada’s footprint with pharmacy benefit manager (PBM) and pharmacy specialty consultants. Partnering closely with the CR Account Executives (CR AEs), who own Omada’s national consulting firm relationships, you will prospect into the PBM and pharmacy practices of our prioritized consulting firms as well as specialty pharmacy consulting and brokerage firms. Your goal is to ensure PBM consultants know Omada, understand our GLP-1 and cardiometabolic solutions, and bring Omada further into the pharmacy and benefit conversations they lead with employers. You will convert those relationships into qualified commercial employer pipeline for our Employer Sales team and help influence consultant advocacy, in collaboration with the CR AEs, on active deals to increase Omada’s win rate.

The successful candidate is a driven, competitive, and consultative seller who understands how PBMs and PBM consultants work, is fluent in the GLP-1 and prescribing landscape, and is energized by opening doors with a specialized consultant audience. This role offers a defined career path to advancement within Omada’s growing commercial sales organization.

About you:

  • 5–7+ years of experience in PBM, pharmacy benefits consulting, employee-benefits sales, consultant relations, or healthcare/digital-health business development, with direct experience engaging PBM or pharmacy consultants serving commercial employers
  • Working knowledge of the PBM market, including PBM organizational structures (sales, account management, clinical, and trade/formulary functions), pricing and contracting models, rebates, formulary management, and utilization management
  • Understanding of how PBM consultants operate—including PBM marketplace RFPs and re-procurements, understanding of formulary and clinical program reviews, and pharmacy benefit strategy for self-funded employers
  • Knowledge of the GLP-1 and prescribing landscape, including GLP-1 coverage and prior authorization decisions, cost and utilization trends, and the role of lifestyle and clinical support programs in managing GLP-1 spend and outcomes
  • Existing relationships within PBM or pharmacy practices at benefits consulting or brokerage firms, and the ability to quickly build new ones across offices and practice teams
  • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales goals, such as meetings set, qualified opportunities generated, or pipeline created
  • Strong prospecting, discovery, and objection-handling skills, with the ability to communicate Omada’s clinical and economic value with credibility to pharmacy-focused consultant audiences
  • Excellent presentation, written, and verbal communication skills
  • Self-starter who can manage a territory or contact portfolio, prioritize effectively, and work with increasing independence
  • Proficiency with Salesforce (or comparable CRM), Google Workspace/Microsoft Office, and sales-engagement tools
  • Competitive, resilient, and coachable—committed to learning, growing, and progressing in healthtech sales

Your impact:

  • Expand Omada’s PBM consultant footprint: Proactively prospect PBM and pharmacy specialty consultants within Omada’s Tier 1 consulting firms and across specialty pharmacy consulting and brokerage firms beyond them
  • Drive new pipeline: Convert PBM consultant relationships into qualified commercial employer opportunities for Omada’s Employer Sales team, with a focus on Omada’s GLP-1 solutions
  • Execute the PBM consultant plan: Own execution of a targeted PBM consultant activation plan aligned with CR AE firm strategies, Employer Sales priorities, and Omada’s GLP-1 growth goals
  • Sell the value: Lead PBM consultant outreach, introductory meetings, and discovery to uncover employer GLP-1 coverage decisions, pharmacy cost pressures, PBM procurement timing, and decision processes, and deliver a compelling Omada value proposition
  • Bridge pharmacy and medical benefits: Connect PBM consultants with their Health & Benefits colleagues and Omada’s CR AEs & Core BDRs so Omada is considered in both pharmacy and medical benefit strategies
  • Help win more business: Partner with CR AEs, Employer Sales, and GLP-1 subject-matter experts to drive PBM consultant advocacy, evaluation inclusion, and positive recommendations on active opportunities
  • Create demand: Plan and execute PBM practice meetings, GLP-1 education sessions, campaigns, and pharmacy industry events designed to produce measurable pipeline
  • Key performance indicators: Success in this role will be measured by (1) PBM consultant meetings and productive engagements held; (2) qualified employer introductions and sales-accepted opportunities generated, including GLP-1 opportunities; (3) PBM consultant-sourced pipeline created ($); (4) PBM consultant-influenced pipeline and win rate on supported opportunities; (5) PBM consultant-sourced and influenced closed business; and (6) growth in active PBM consultant relationships across Tier 1 and specialty firms
  • Supporting responsibilities: Maintain accurate Salesforce activity, contact, and opportunity attribution; build PBM practice relationship maps; contribute to CR reporting; help keep PBM- and GLP-1-related consultant resources and RFI/RFP content current; share PBM market and competitive insights; and support CR projects as needed
  • Role requires travel (35%)

About Omada Health: Omada Health (Nasdaq: OMDA) is reverse engineering the way healthcare is delivered in America, putting the space between doctor visits–where health is won or lost–at the center of care. Today's healthcare system poorly serves chronic conditions that require ongoing support outside of the exam room, like obesity, diabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, and musculoskeletal conditions. Omada’s virtual-first model combines human-led care teams, connected devices, and AI-enabled technology to deliver personalized care at scale, including support for GLP-1 therapy. Omada has served more than two million members since launch across 2,000+ employers, health plans, pharmacy benefit managers, and health systems. Learn more at omadahealth.com.
