Pharmacy Technician, Call Center Operations Trainer

Mid-level · NJ - Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Sep 22, 2026Workday

AnewHealth is one of the nation’s leading pharmacy care management companies that specializes in caring for people with the most complex, chronic needs—wherever they call home. We enable better outcomes for patients and the healthcare organizations who support them. Established in 2023 through the combination of ExactCare and Tabula Rasa HealthCare, we provide a suite of solutions that includes comprehensive pharmacy services; full-service pharmacy benefit management; and specialized support services for Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly. With over 1,400 team members, we care for more than 100,000 people across all 50 states.

Job Details

The CareKinesis Pharmacy Technician, Call Center Operations Trainer should have a broad subject matter expertise, have consistently met performance expectations and have demonstrated the ability to train and develop team members on tasks required for the department workflow. The CareKinesis Call Center Operations Trainer will continue to do critical front-line work and will be a key contributor to the department workflow. The CareKinesis Call Center Operations Trainer will conduct continued training for new employees after their introductory period of training through our Organizational Development department has been completed. We view this position as a developmental role in providing an opportunity for a strong contributor with the ability to explain tasks and train others to take a step forward in their career and provide an opportunity to demonstrate higher level leadership abilities.

Responsibilities

  • High level of contribution in front line, day-to-day work as assigned by leads and supervisors

  • Provide training on department tasks to new hires, cross trainees and existing team members requiring additional support

  • Help coordinate the completion of cross training through communication and scheduling with designated trainees

  • Provide a high level of subject matter expertise to the department in supporting continuous improvement projects

  • Support leads and supervisors in diagnosing and improving performance of team members

Requirements

  • Must be 18+ years of age. High school diploma /GED required.

  • Must have Pharmacy Technician experience.

  • Certified with the Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB) or the National Health Association (NHA) and maintain a current certificate, required.

  • Must have an active Pharmacy Technician license in the state residing and obtain the New Jersey Pharmacy Technician license within 6-months of hire.

  • Flexibility to adapt schedule as needed, including evenings or weekends, to meet the demands of training and operational priorities

  • Ability to perform in an office based (or remote) setting that requires sitting at a desk 90% of the time.

Physical/Mental Demands: This position is administrative in nature and will present physical demands requisite to a position requiring: hearing, seeing, sitting, standing, talking, and walking. The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this job. May be necessary to work extended hours as needed.

Schedule: This is a full-time position with an expectation to work an average of 40 hours per week and be available outside of normal business hours to meet customer expectations on an ad-hoc basis. Schedules are set to accommodate the requirements of the position and the needs of the organization and may be adjusted as needed.

This is a remote position.
