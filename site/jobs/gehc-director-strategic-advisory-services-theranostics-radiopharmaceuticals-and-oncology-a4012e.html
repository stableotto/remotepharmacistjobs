Director, Strategic Advisory Services - Theranostics, Radiopharmaceuticals, and Oncology

Senior · $156,800 - $235,200/yr · Remote
RemoteSeniorPosted Oct 23, 2026Workday

Job Description Summary

GE HealthCare’s Strategic Advisory Services team partners with health systems to improve patient outcomes, operational performance, financial sustainability, and strategic growth through expertise-led, technology-enabled care transformation.

We are seeking a deeply experienced Theranostics leader to serve as a domain expert within our national healthcare advisory team. Working alongside experienced strategy and operations consultants, this individual will help health systems design, launch, scale, and optimize Theranostics programs and associated oncology care pathways.

The successful candidate will bring credible, hands-on understanding of radiopharmaceutical therapy, molecular imaging, multidisciplinary care delivery, and the operational, regulatory, and financial requirements of successful Theranostics programs. This expert-practitioner will translate deep domain knowledge into executive-level strategies, practical recommendations, and implementation road maps that create measurable customer value.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities 

Theranostics and Executive Advisory 

  • Serve as the team’s lead Theranostics subject-matter expert and a trusted advisor to health system executives, physicians, and clinical, operational, and financial leaders. 
  • Advise clients on the clinical, operational, organizational, regulatory, facility, and financial considerations involved in developing and sustaining high-quality Theranostics programs. 
  • Translate complex Theranostics considerations into clear strategic choices, practical recommendations, and implementation priorities. 
  • Maintain current knowledge of relevant clinical evidence, emerging indications, regulatory developments, reimbursement policy, technologies, and care-delivery models. 

Client Engagement and Delivery 

  • Lead on Theranostics and Molecular Imaging advisory engagements focused on program development, growth, transformation, and performance improvement. 
  • Translate deep Theranostics expertise into recommendations that improve patient access, care delivery, organizational performance, and program sustainability. 
  • Assess client capabilities, performance, opportunities, and barriers to successful Theranostics program development. 
  • Facilitate executive and physician working sessions that build alignment and support informed decision-making. 
  • Integrate clinical, operational, strategic, and financial considerations to support complex health system decisions. 
  • Structure engagements and deliver executive-quality analyses, recommendations, and implementation plans. 
  • Manage projects with rigor and risk management to deliver on-time and on-budget. 

Practice and Market Development 

  • Contribute Theranostics and Molecular Imaging expertise to the continued development and improvement of Strategic Advisory Services capabilities and client work. 
  • Support business development through customer discovery, engagement scoping, proposal development, and executive presentations. 
  • Contribute to relevant thought leadership, professional forums, and relationships within the Theranostics community. 
  • Collaborate across GE HealthCare and contribute Theranostics and molecular imaging expertise to broader oncology and adjacent care-transformation engagements, as appropriate.  
  • Build trusted relationships with health system executives, physicians, clinical leaders, and industry stakeholders. 
  • Contribute to market research, conference presentations, articles, white papers, executive briefings, and other thought-leadership activities. 
  • Represent Strategic Advisory Services in relevant customer, professional, and industry forums. 

 

Required Qualifications 

  • Bachelor’s degree in nuclear medicine, molecular imaging, clinical sciences, medical physics, healthcare administration, business, or related field. 
  • Progressive leadership experience in nuclear medicine, molecular imaging, oncology, radiopharmaceutical therapy, healthcare operations, or a closely related field. 
  • Substantial direct experience designing, launching, operating, expanding, or optimizing a Theranostics or radiopharmaceutical therapy program. 
  • Deep understanding of the Theranostics patient journey and the clinical, operational, regulatory, infrastructure, facility, staffing, and financial requirements of successful program delivery. 
  • Demonstrated ability to advise senior executives, physicians, and cross-functional health system leaders with credibility. 
  • Ability to translate complex clinical and operational issues into structured analyses, executive-level recommendations, and actionable plans. 
  • Experience leading complex, cross-functional initiatives through ambiguity, organizational change, and competing stakeholder priorities. 
  • Strong analytical skills, experience with healthcare data, and able to find impactful insights in complex data sets. 
  • Strong communication, facilitation, and executive presentation skills. 
  • Self-directed and intellectually curious, with the ability to lead complex work and manage multiple priorities in an evolving healthcare environment. 
  • Willingness to travel up to 50%. 

 

Preferred Qualifications 

  • Advanced degree or relevant professional credentials in nuclear medicine, molecular imaging, medical physics, radiation safety, oncology, healthcare administration, or related discipline. 
  • Direct experience with PSMA-targeted and somatostatin receptor-targeted diagnostic and therapeutic pathways. 
  • Experience addressing multiple dimensions of Theranostics program development, including care pathways, program operations, infrastructure, regulatory readiness, and financial sustainability. 
  • Experience with radiopharmaceutical therapy clinical trials, emerging indications, or evolving care-delivery models. 
  • External thought leadership through publications, conference presentations, professional societies, research, or industry working groups. 
  • Experience in healthcare consulting, advisory services, enterprise transformation, or performance improvement.
