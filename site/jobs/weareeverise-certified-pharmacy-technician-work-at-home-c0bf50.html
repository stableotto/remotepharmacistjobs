Do you enjoy helping people and making a meaningful impact on their health and well-being?
Everise has an exciting opportunity for Certified Pharmacy Technicians (CPhT) who are
passionate about supporting patients and healthcare professionals. In this role, you will utilize
your pharmacy knowledge to assist with medication-related inquiries, support prescription
processing activities, and provide exceptional customer service to patients. Join a team
dedicated to improving healthcare experiences while helping individuals navigate their
pharmacy and medication needs with confidence.

Everise offers unique opportunities to our candidates:
• Competitive pay

• Full time positions include a robust benefit program including medical, dental, vision,
401k plans and PTO.
• Excellent career pathing- We promote from within!
Does this sound good to you? If so, we ask that you bring the following:
• Education Requirements - High School Diploma or GED
• Active TX Pharmacy Technician/Trainee License
• Strong detail orientation and excellent communication/listening skills
• Strong computer skills that help you to efficiently maneuver through multiple applications
simultaneously
• Authorization to work in the United States
We would love if you had:
• Minimum of 6 months experience in a remote call center environment
• National Pharmacy Technician Certification (CPhT)
• Pharmacy, call center, or member services experience
• Knowledge of diabetic supplies and pharmacy operations
Everise is committed to keeping our employees and our workplaces safe during these uncertain
times. For now, most of our positions are remote as part of our work-at-home program.
