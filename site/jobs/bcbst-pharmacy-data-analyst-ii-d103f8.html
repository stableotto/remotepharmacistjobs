Pharmacy Data Analyst II

Mid-level · USA, TN, Chattanooga, Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Jun 18, 2026Workday

We are hiring a Pharmacy Data Analyst II at BCBST!

In this role, you will support advanced pharmacy analytics and reporting efforts within a modern, cloud-based environment, contributing directly to the team’s ongoing SAS-to-Databricks migration and broader analytics transformation. You will play a key role in testing and validation to ensure data accuracy and reliability, while supporting AI-enabled analytics, infrastructure enhancements, and ongoing maintenance of critical business deliverables. You’ll partner with cross-functional teams to deliver scalable, data-driven insights, fulfill custom reporting requests, and actively contribute to AI initiatives through hands-on involvement in agent training and optimization. Your work will be essential to maintaining delivery quality, meeting SAS decommissioning timelines, and advancing high-impact, innovative analytics across the organization.

To be successful in this role, in addition to core job requirements, you’ll bring strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and experience working in cloud-based data environments. You will be a strong candidate if you have experience with SQL and Databricks, a background in data validation and testing, and familiarity with supporting analytics infrastructure or large-scale data platform transitions.

Note:

  • This is remote, work-from-home position, but the final round of interviews will take place on-site in our Chattanooga, TN office.
  • SQL and Databricks experience preferred.
  • Experience with AI agent training, development, or optimization is strongly preferred.
  • Sponsorship is not available for this role.

Job Responsibilities

  • Analyzing, defining requirements, developing, and validating complex reporting and/or data files necessary to meet business objectives.
  • Utilizing business knowledge to perform in-depth analyses, proactively investigating abnormal results, writing descriptive summaries of gathered data, and optimizing delivery of end product.
  • Proactively analyzing changes in business processes and updating reporting/file feeds accordingly.
  • Researching and responding to questions and change requests from business partners.
  • Articulating reporting results and business impact.
  • Peer review work product of junior and senior analysts and mentor junior analysts.  
     

Job Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelors degree in business or healthcare or relevant field or equivalent work experience required

Experience

  • 3 years - Experience with SAS (Base, Enterprise Guide, Studio), SQL, Tableau, or similar experience with programming and/or statistical analysis required
  • 5 years - Reporting, data analysis, or similar experience required.

Skills\Certifications

  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision or function in a team environment sharing responsibility, roles and accountability.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office (Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint)
  • Proficient interpersonal and organizational skills
  • Capacity to solve problems and manage multiple assignments with critical deadlines
  • Strong analytical skills
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities
  • Adaptive to high pace and changing environment
