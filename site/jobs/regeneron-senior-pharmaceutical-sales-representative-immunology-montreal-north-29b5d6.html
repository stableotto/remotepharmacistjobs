Senior Pharmaceutical Sales Representative Immunology - Montreal North

Senior · Remote - Canada
RemoteSeniorPosted Oct 13, 2026Workday

At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Senior Pharmaceutical Sales Representative Immunology Montreal North to join our commercial team, supporting our Québec territory in a field-based work mode. In this role, you will drive commercial objectives through strategic territory management and healthcare professional engagement while collaborating with medical colleagues and cross-functional teams. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.


When & where:

Montreal North, Canada

Field-based


Discover your role:


  • Lead high-quality interactions with Healthcare Professionals (HCPs).
  • Deliver brand value messages aligned with sales and marketing plans.
  • Develop local account plans using key data and market insights.
  • Manage territory planning, targeting, segmentation, and key accounts.
  • Partner with medical colleagues on regional programs supporting HCP needs.
  • Leverage face-to-face, remote, email, and digital engagement channels.
  • Report territory results and developments to Immunology leadership.
  • You thrive in a collaborative environment and build strong partnerships.

This role requires:


  • Bachelor’s degree.
  • 5+ years of pharmaceutical sales experience.
  • 1-2 years of specialty biologic experience.
  • Experience in Immunology; Dermatology experience preferred.
  • Ability to run territory planning, account management, targeting, and segmentation.
  • Experience working across multiple customer engagement channels.
  • Valid driver’s license and willingness to travel regularly.
  • French and English language proficiency.
  • Strong collaboration skills with internal and external partners; alliance experience is helpful.
  • Ability to operate optimally within a newly established team environment.
