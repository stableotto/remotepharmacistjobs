What you will do: 

Formulary & Utilization Management

  • Strategy Development: Design clinically sound UM tools and client-specific solutions to support cost-effective formulary structures.
  • Maintenance & QA: Maintain the accuracy of drug formularies across all platforms through rigorous testing and quality assurance.
  • Clinical & Financial Review: Evaluate new drug products and drug classes to recommend positioning based on rebate compliance, market trends, and clinical evidence.

Data Analysis & Insights

  • Claims Analysis: Audit claims data to identify cost-savings opportunities and trends that require clinical intervention.
  • Strategic Optimization: Translate data into actionable insights to enhance formulary performance and reduce healthcare costs.

Pipeline & P&T Committee Support

  • Pipeline Evaluation: Monitor and assess the potential formulary impact of upcoming drug pipelines.
  • Committee Coordination: Lead activities for the Pharmacy & Therapeutics (P&T) committee, including preparing drug reviews and executing formulary updates.

Collaboration & Communication

  • Internal SME: Act as the primary clinical expert for internal operations, implementation teams, and external partners.
  • Content Creation: Develop and update clinical marketing materials and formulary communications for diverse stakeholders.

What you will bring to SmithRx:

  • Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) required.
  • Minimum of 2 years in managed care or PBM (with a focus on formulary/operations) OR completion of a managed care residency.
  • Proven knowledge of pharmacy benefit management (PBM) practices, including UM and clinical program design.
  • Expertise in Medispan drug file systems and advanced MS Office skills (specifically Excel and Access).
  • Ability to prioritize multiple high-stakes tasks in a fast-paced, evolving corporate environment.
  • Exceptional interpersonal, written, and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong critical thinking skills with the ability to exercise independent judgment when solving complex problems.
