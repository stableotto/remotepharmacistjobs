Manager, Pharmacy Services

Senior · USA, TN, Chattanooga, Remote
RemoteSeniorPosted Aug 27, 2026Workday

We are hiring a Manager, Pharmacy Business Services at BCBST!

In this role, you will provide leadership across key pharmacy business services functions, helping translate enterprise priorities into effective execution. You will be responsible for overseeing the implementation of enterprise pharmacy initiatives, managing budgets and financial performance, leading a complex portfolio of vendor relationships, and driving contract lifecycle management and governance activities. This position plays a critical role in maintaining strong business oversight, accountability, and organizational alignment while supporting the successful delivery of enterprise pharmacy initiatives, business management functions, and shared services activities across multiple stakeholders. As a people leader, you will lead and develop a team of analysts, fostering collaboration, professional growth, and a culture of accountability and results.

 

Our ideal candidates for this role will have: 

  • Strong business management experience, financial acumen, and a proven ability to drive execution in a complex environment.
  • Knowledge of vendor contracting processes and healthcare project management methodologies.
  • Minimum of 7 years experience in the Healthcare Industry.
  • Experience managing budgets, contracts, vendors, projects, governance processes, and business planning activities.
  • Experience overseeing large vendor portfolios, managing contract lifecycles, driving accountability, facilitating cross-functional initiatives, and leading teams through change will be highly valued.

 

Candidates who can balance strategic priorities with strong business oversight, sound decision-making, and effective execution will thrive in this role.

 

Note:

  • This is a remote, work-from-home position.
  • Final-round interviews will take place on-site at the Chattanooga Office.
  • Sponsorship is not available for this role.

Job Responsibilities

  • Strategic Execution: Translate enterprise pharmacy strategy into operational plans and lead execution across assigned business units.
  • Team Leadership: Foster a high-performing team culture focused on accountability, engagement, and continuous improvement.
  • Escalation Management: Resolve complex issues and ensure efficient delivery of pharmacy services across multiple functions.
  • Performance Management: Establish and monitor KPIs, identify performance gaps, and lead initiatives to improve outcomes and efficiency.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with clinical, finance, IT, and vendor teams to align pharmacy services with broader enterprise initiatives.
  • Audit Readiness: Lead operational readiness for audits and enterprise reviews, ensuring documentation and processes meet quality standards.
  • Compliance Management: Ensure pharmacy business activities meet regulatory and contractual obligations.

Job Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience required. Equivalent work experience is defined as 4 years of professional work experience.

Experience

  • Minimum 7 years Healthcare Industry experience required
  • 3 years - Supervisory/leadership experience required

Skills\Certifications

  • Ability to execute programs and operational plans
  • Skill in team leadership and talent development
  • Expertise in issue resolution and problem-solving
  • Strong situational awareness and emotional intelligence
  • Capability to collaborate across clinical, finance, and vendor teams
