Job Description
 

The Consulting Pharmacist at Fuze Health plays a vital role in delivering safe, accurate, and patient-centered pharmaceutical services. Operating as an individual contributor, this role works cross-functionally with teams across the organization to support pharmacy operations, ensure regulatory compliance, and provide high-quality patient care.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Team Support & Oversight: Assist in the supervision of pharmacy technicians as needed to ensure efficient workflow and operational excellence.
  • Service Coordination: Coordinate the provision of pharmaceutical services, including the delivery of medications and products to patients, ensuring timely and accurate fulfillment.
  • Documentation & Compliance: Maintain all required documentation for services rendered and ensure adherence to local, state, and federal regulations.
  • Drug Security: Safeguard legend drugs and ensure proper handling, storage, and security in accordance with regulatory standards.
  • Quality Assurance: Participate in the pharmacy quality assurance program to drive continuous improvement in service delivery and patient safety.
  • Patient & Provider Communication: Regularly correspond with patients and healthcare providers via telephone, offering medication consultations and ensuring exceptional service.
  • Education & Guidance: Support the Pharmacy Operations Leader in educating pharmacy staff and staying current on regulatory guidelines and industry standards.
  • Patient Confidentiality: Maintain strict confidentiality of patient profiles, medication information, and all sensitive health data.

Required Experience & Qualifications:

  • Active, clear, and unrestricted Pharmacist license in the State of Ohio.
  • Registered Pharmacist in good standing with the Ohio Board of Pharmacy.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD).
  • Minimum of 1 year of experience as a Registered Pharmacist.
  • Ability and willingness to work at a computer screen for extended periods.
  • Strong organizational and leadership skills to support pharmacy operations.
  • A deep commitment to putting patients first and upholding high ethical standards.
