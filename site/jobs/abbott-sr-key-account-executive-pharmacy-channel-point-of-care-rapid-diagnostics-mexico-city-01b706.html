The Opportunity

We’re empowering smarter medical and economic decision making to help transform the way people manage their health at all stages of life. Every day, more than 10 million tests are run on Abbott’s diagnostics instruments, providing lab results for millions of people. Our rapid diagnostics solutions are helping address some of the world’s greatest healthcare challenges.

This position Sr Key Account Executive Pharmacy Channel Point Of Care out of our Mexico City Office in our Rapid Diagnostics Division (ARDx) is responsable to Develop ARDx pharmacy chain business through the opening of new and potential business opportunities with strategic accounts. To develop this business, require business analytics, strategic thinking, anticipate epidemiologic trends and manage relationship with stakeholders C-suite level. Our rapid diagnostics solutions are helping address some of the world’s greatest healthcare challenges.

What You’ll Do:

  • Develop the pharmacy and retail channels leading conversations with strategic and potential customers to create long term and scalable business.
  • Introduce products with customers creating proposals and negotiation of contracts considering terms and conditions to protect ARDx business interest.
  • Forecast management to guarantee product availability to achieve customer needs and business goals anticipating epidemiological trends.
  • Propose and launch marketing and trade marketing initiatives to increase brand awareness.
  • Stakeholder mapping and management making C-suite level business relationship to introduce POC business.
  • Full account management of direct and indirect sales through all administrative process to fulfill customer needs. Negotiating PO’s, customer service, fill rate, accounts payable, rebates and returns.
  • Develop and manage sales funnel using sales force. 

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s in Administration, Economics, Marketing or related areas.
  • Experience minimum with 2 years in sales with wholesalers, distributors, pharmacy chains. Introducing new market and category opportunities. Negotiating contracts, prices and commercial conditions with customers.
  • Account Mapping and key stakeholder management.
  • +3 years of experience in pharma, rapid diagnostics, medical devices preferred.
  • Experience with Sales Force
  • English Advanced
  • Ability to travel  of working time away from work location, may include overnight/weekend travel.
  • Positive can-do attitude
  • Open New Businesses
  • Public Relations.
