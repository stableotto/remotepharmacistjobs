The Mail Operations Pharmacy Technician 3 performs advanced administrative/operational/customer support duties that require independent initiative and judgment. May apply intermediate mathematical skills.

The Dr Call Phones Pharmacy Technician 3 works from home in a call center capacity and is responsible for the accurate and timely in-take of prescription transfers. The Dr. Call Phones Pharmacy Technician 3 work assignments involve moderately complex issues where the analysis of situations or data requires an in-depth evaluation of dispensing guidelines. 

In this role you will: 

  • Make outbound calls/ take inbound calls to obtain prescription transfers from pharmacies 
  • Check and verify drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity, refills, and directions 


Use your skills to make an impact
 

Preferred Qualifications 

  • 1 year minimum of Mail Order Pharmacy or Retail Pharmacy experience 
  • Experience working with insurance plans and Medicare
