Job Details

**Applicants must reside in Ohio or Florida

Join the Cleveland Clinic team where you will work alongside passionate caregivers and provide patient-first healthcare. Here, you will receive endless support and appreciation while building a rewarding career with one of the most respected healthcare organizations in the world.   

The EBI Analyst II – Pharmacy supports Enterprise Business Intelligence initiatives for Cleveland Clinic Pharmacy, with a focus on Specialty Pharmacy and broader operations. This role plays a critical part in ensuring medication access, regulatory compliance, and data-driven decision-making by delivering analytics for financial, operational, clinical, and research needs, including supporting drug distributor reporting tied to significant revenue. Working closely with pharmacists, pharmacy leadership, analytics teams, and cross-functional stakeholders, the analyst is responsible for extracting and analyzing large datasets, writing and optimizing SQL queries, and developing reports and dashboards. With a balanced workload of project-based and ad hoc requests, this position offers high visibility, autonomy, and the opportunity to translate complex data into actionable insights that directly impact patient care and pharmacy operations.

A caregiver in this position resides in Ohio, Florida or Nevada works days from 8:00AM – 5:00PM.

A caregiver who excels in this role will:  

  • Facilitate the creation of new operational indicators by identifying process improvement opportunities and utilizing disparate data sources to provide timely, accurate and meaningful decision support tools. 
  • Utilize computer skills to maintain complex reporting systems essential to providing timely and accurate reporting. 
  • Become an expert in at least two Cleveland Clinic subject matters, such as patient occupancy, patient experience, operating room utilization, Professional Coding and physician productivity. 
  • Serve as a technical resource in the analysis and evaluation of operations and financial functions of clinical areas as related to utilization management and operational indicator performance. 
  • Interpret and present data analysis results, including explanation of statistical and financial techniques used, assumptions made and summary of results. 
  • Document methods and interpretation of results for future reference. 
  • Create summary and detail reports for online dashboards and other platforms for all levels of the Cleveland Clinic utilizing dashboard software. 
  • Ensure the quality and data integrity of reports, making improvements and adjustments as needed. 
  • Provide guidance to level I Analysts when needed. 

Minimum qualifications for the ideal future caregiver include:  

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field and two years of related experience  
  • OR High School Diploma/GED and six years of database development or analytical experience  
  • OR Associate’s Degree and four years of database development or analytical experience  
  • OR Master’s Degree and one year of experience 
  • Strong computer skills and be proficient with various applications including, Access, Excel and PowerPoint 
  • Understanding of data warehousing and t-SQL Programming 

Preferred qualifications for the ideal future caregiver include:  

  • Experience with Business Objects
  • Pharmacy and/or healthcare experience 
  • Experience working with large, complex datasets
  • Background in data analytics or related field
  • Ability to translate business needs into technical solutions

Physical Requirements:

  • Ability to communicate and exchange accurate information. 
  • Ability to perform work in a stationary position for extended periods.  
  • Ability to work with physical records or operate a computer or other office equipment.  
  • In some locations, ability to travel throughout the hospital system.  
  • In some locations ability to move up to 25 lbs.

Personal Protective Equipment:
