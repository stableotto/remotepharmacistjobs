Pharmacy Network Coordinator

Mid-level · $70,000 - $91,000/yr · Remote
RemoteMid-levelPosted Sep 17, 2026Greenhouse

ABOUT THE ROLE:

The Pharmacy Network Coordinator supports the day-to-day management of Rightway's retail, mail-order, and specialty pharmacy networks. This person keeps network data accurate, coordinates pharmacy onboarding and credentialing, and works cross-functionally with claims, client success, and clinical teams to make sure members always have reliable access to the pharmacies they need.

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

  • Maintains and audits pharmacy network directories, contracts, and credentialing records for accuracy and compliance.  This includes obtaining applicable licenses and certificates of insurance from pharmacies, as well as verifying eligibility through the NCPDP database and federal and state sources (e.g., OIG, GSA, SAM).
  • Coordinates onboarding, terminations, and status changes for retail, mail-order, and specialty pharmacies.
  • Serves as a day-to-day point of contact for network pharmacies, resolving claims, enrollment, and access issues.
  • Partners with the claims and clinical teams to troubleshoot pharmacy-level adjudication and payment issues. 
  • Supports the benefit and navigation team with network-related issues. 
  • Develops and delivers ad hoc network reports for clients.
  • Helps document and continuously improve network operations processes.

WHO YOU ARE:

  • 2+ years of experience in pharmacy benefits, PBM operations, network management, or a related healthcare role.
  • Working knowledge of pharmacy network contracting, credentialing, or claims adjudication.
  • Ability to work independently, with strong attention to detail and comfort managing data across multiple systems.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a service-first approach to pharmacy partners.
  • Proven track record in fast-paced environments, managing multiple priorities simultaneously and bringing the initiative and persistence needed to drive outreach to pharmacy providers and close out contracts and tasks.
  • Intermediate Proficiency in Word, Excel or Google Sheets; experience with PBM or claims platforms is a plus.

EXTRA CREDIT:

  • Familiarity with PBM regulatory and network adequacy requirements.
  • Background working directly with retail, mail-order, or specialty pharmacies.
  • PTCB certification (or state license).

COMPENSATION:  $70,000 - $91,000 annually, in addition to bonus and equity

Compensation offered will be determined by geographic location, experience, and qualifications.

ABOUT RIGHTWAY:

Rightway is on a mission to harmonize healthcare for everyone, everywhere. Our products guide patients to the best care and medications by inserting clinicians and pharmacists into a patient’s care journey through a modern, mobile app. Rightway is a front door to healthcare, giving patients the tools they need along with on-demand access to Rightway health guides, human experts that answer their questions and manage the frustrating parts of healthcare for them.

Since its founding in 2017, Rightway has raised over $200mm from investors including Khosla Ventures, Thrive Capital, and Tiger Global. We’re headquartered in New York City, with satellite offices in Denver and Dallas. Our clients rely on us to transform the healthcare experience, improve outcomes for their teams, and decrease their healthcare costs.
